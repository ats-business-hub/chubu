Takayama 2-day itinerary

One night in Takayama, with an optional Shirakawa-go morning. Official pages first.

Two days is enough for Takayama’s old town plus one outing, if you arrive from Nagoya on the Limited Express Hida and spend the night. It is tighter if you also want Matsumoto, Kamikochi, or the Shinhotaka Ropeway — those are better as extra days or as swaps, not as add-ons to the same 48 hours. Recheck seasonal closures (Kamikochi is not a winter option) on official pages.

How to get there

Start at Nagoya Station and take JR’s Limited Express Hida to Takayama. Recheck today’s timetable and fare on official JR Central pages before you buy. If you will walk the old town on arrival, consider sending a bag ahead or using station lockers; check locker sizes and availability at the station.

The Takayama–Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass can make sense if this two-day stay is part of the wider Kanazawa–Takayama loop. It is not the nationwide Japan Rail Pass. Compare coverage, exchange, and price on the official seller page before any third-party cart.

A simple two-day shape

Day 1 — Arrive and walk the old town

  • Arrive from Nagoya on the Hida and walk or bus to your stay.
  • Spend the afternoon in the Sanmachi / old-town streets and, if you want a structured walk, compare an optional in-town tour after you have already looked at the official tourism pages.
  • Keep the evening in town. The official Shirakawa Village page says night sightseeing in the gassho village is not available to day visitors, so do not plan a late bus “just to see the lights” unless you have a booked village stay.

Day 2 — Morning markets, then Shirakawa-go or Hida Folk Village

Takayama has two morning markets. Official hours, checked 21 November 2026:

  • Miyagawa Morning Market (宮川朝市) is held every day from 7:00 to 12:00, and from 8:00 to 12:00 in winter (December through March). The official page notes that setup and packing-up happen within those hours, and that fewer stalls may open in bad weather.
  • Jinya-mae Morning Market (陣屋前朝市) is year-round: 6:00 to 12:00 from April through December, and 7:00 to 12:00 from January through March.

Both officially close at noon. Recheck those pages before you set an alarm, because stall hours can still vary.

  • If you want Shirakawa-go, use a morning highway bus from Takayama Bus Terminal. The official Nohi page says the ride takes about 50 minutes, with 16 round trips a day, and that some buses require a reservation. Sample weekday-pattern departures on the timetable valid through 30 November 2026 include 7:20, 7:50, 8:10, and 8:50, arriving at Shirakawa-go (Ogimachi) at 8:10, 8:40, 9:00, and 9:40. Recheck the live table; some services are reservation-only or do not run every day.
  • Give yourself several hours in the village, follow the official manners page, and return to Takayama in time for a Hida back to Nagoya. Nohi warns that delays of two hours or more can occur.

If you would rather not spend Day 2 on a highway bus, stay in Takayama. Hida Folk Village (Hida no Sato), the official outdoor museum, lists opening hours of 8:30–17:00, open year-round. Admission, checked 21 November 2026 on the official site: adults 700 yen (tax included); children (elementary and junior-high school) 200 yen; groups of 20 or more, 600 yen / 150 yen. The official page says holders of a disability certificate enter free (the holder only). All-day parking: regular cars and small or medium buses 300 yen; large buses 1,000 yen. Recheck the official site for evening light-up dates, when hours and tickets differ, and for any reserved-event days.

If you have a third day

Better as a swap or an extra night, not as a squeeze into two days: Kamikochi (the live hub notes the official season is mid-April to mid-November; recheck), the Shinhotaka Ropeway (official fares; service can stop for weather), or Matsumoto Castle. Frequently asked questions

When do the morning markets open?
Miyagawa Morning Market is officially daily, 7:00–12:00, but 8:00–12:00 from December through March. Jinya-mae is officially 6:00–12:00 from April through December and 7:00–12:00 in January–March. Both close at noon. Recheck the official pages.
Is one night in Takayama enough?
Yes, for the old town, the morning markets, and an optional morning bus to Shirakawa-go. Build slack into the bus, especially after 1 December 2026 when fares and some services change.
