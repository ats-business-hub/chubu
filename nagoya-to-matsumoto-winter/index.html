Nagoya to Matsumoto by train in winter: Limited Express Shinano (2026–27)

Times and ticket rules from JR Central and Visit Matsumoto, checked 26 November 2026. Not a hands-on test. Shinano fares are not listed here, because they were not printed on the official pages checked for this article.

Some links on this page are affiliate links for Viator, Sakura Mobile, and Japan Wireless; if you book through them I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Official railway pages remain the place to check times and buy tickets. #ad

The usual winter route from Nagoya to Matsumoto is JR Central’s Limited Express Shinano on the Chuo Line. Visit Matsumoto says the journey from the Nagoya area takes about two hours on the Shinano, with no transfer. This article does not invent a regular-season timetable or a fare. Check live times on JR Central’s timetable search, or with Visit Matsumoto, before you travel.

Winter 2026–27: extra Shinano trains and all-reserved seats

JR Central’s release dated 22 October 2026 (PDF 000046107) says it will run a total of 18 extra Limited Express Shinano trains to the Matsumoto and Nagano area, mainly around the year-end and New Year period. The same PDF lists these extra trains:

From 25 December to 5 January, JR Central will operate Shinano, Hida and Nanki as all-reserved trains, with no non-reserved seats, “to increase the chance of getting a seat.” Reserved limited-express tickets go on sale at 10:00 a.m. one month before the travel date (the same day of the previous month) at main JR stations. Express Reservation and Smart EX, which are for the Tokaido, Sanyo and Kyushu Shinkansen, can book reserved seats up to one year ahead; that one-year window is not described as applying to the Shinano.

Tickets

You need a base fare ticket plus a limited-express ticket. Buy them from official JR channels. This page does not list a Shinano fare, because the official pages and the winter extra-train PDF checked on 26 November 2026 did not print one. A Japan Rail Pass may cover the base fare, but Visit Matsumoto notes that a separate reserved-seat limited-express ticket is still required if you want a reserved seat. Confirm the current pass rules on the official Japan Rail Pass site before you travel.

Luggage

The oversized-baggage reservation rule is for Tokaido, Sanyo, Kyushu and Nishi Kyushu Shinkansen trains, not for conventional-line trains. JR Central’s oversized-baggage FAQ says reservations are not required to bring oversized baggage onto conventional-line trains the way they are on the Shinkansen. The Shinano is a conventional-line limited express, so that Shinkansen reservation rule does not apply to the Shinano itself.

If part of your trip is on the Tokaido Shinkansen, the Shinkansen rules do apply on that segment: items with a total of three dimensions between 161 and 250 cm need a reserved oversized-baggage space; boarding with oversized baggage and no reservation is charged 1,000 yen including tax; baggage over 250 cm is not allowed in that scheme. You may still carry on two pieces of up to 30 kg each, with total dimensions of 250 cm or less and a maximum length of 200 cm, if you follow the reserved-space rules where they apply. Strollers, sports equipment and musical instruments are outside the oversized-baggage rule, though a reservation is required if you want to use the oversized space. Recheck the official page before you travel.

Timetables and service status

Use JR Central’s official timetable search and the conventional-line service status page for delays. This article does not list the regular (non-extra) Shinano times, because they were not confirmed from a current official winter timetable.

Winter in Matsumoto, according to the city

Visit Matsumoto describes winter events and access in its official English guides. On driving, Visit Matsumoto says studless tires must be installed on vehicles by law, and it advises travellers not to use a rental car if they have no experience of snowy roads. That wording is Visit Matsumoto’s; this page does not independently interpret the statute. If you plan to drive, confirm current rules with your rental company and local guidance.

From Matsumoto you can visit Matsumoto Castle. Kamikochi from Matsumoto is a different trip and is seasonal; see Kamikochi day trip from Matsumoto. For a winter day further into Nagano, see Jigokudani snow monkeys from Nagoya.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the Shinano take from Nagoya to Matsumoto?
Visit Matsumoto says the journey from the Nagoya area takes about two hours on the Limited Express Shinano, with no transfer. This page does not list a regular-season timetable; check JR Central’s official search before you travel.
Are there extra Shinano trains at New Year?
Yes. JR Central’s 22 October 2026 release says it will run 18 extra Shinano trains to the Matsumoto and Nagano area, mainly around the year-end period. It lists Shinano 85 (Nagoya 10:30–Matsumoto 12:57, 26–30 December) and Shinano 83 (Nagoya 8:12–Nagano 11:27, 1–4 January), plus return trains 82 and 86.
Is the Shinano all reserved at New Year?
From 25 December to 5 January, JR Central will operate Shinano, Hida and Nanki as all-reserved trains, with no non-reserved seats.
Do I need an oversized-baggage reservation on the Shinano?
JR Central says the advance-reservation rule for oversized baggage applies to Tokaido, Sanyo, Kyushu and Nishi Kyushu Shinkansen trains, not to conventional-line trains. The Shinano is a conventional-line limited express. If part of your trip is on the Tokaido Shinkansen, the Shinkansen rules apply on that segment.
