Pocket Wi-Fi, SIM, or eSIM at Chubu Centrair Airport

The airport post office handles pickups only on weekdays from 9 to 5. Here is what to book for when you land.

If you fly into Chubu Centrair International Airport (NGO) for Nagoya, Takayama, or Kanazawa, you can be online before you reach the train. The catch is where and when you collect a pre-booked router or SIM. Two of the companies below hand Centrair orders over at the airport post office, and that post office closes at 5 pm and does not open at weekends or on Japanese public holidays. Everything below was checked on official pages on 25 November 2026.

Related: eSIM vs pocket Wi-Fi for Chubu · Japan eSIM for Central Japan · Centrair Airport to Nagoya · Centrair Airport to Takayama.

The short version

  • Phone takes an eSIM? Buy one before you fly. There is nothing to collect, and the airport’s hours stop mattering.
  • Landing on a weekday between 9 am and 5 pm? Post-office pickup (Sakura Mobile, Japan Wireless) works. Land outside those hours and you need a different pickup point.
  • Landing at night or at the weekend? Book a counter that keeps longer hours. NINJA WiFi shares the Global WiFi counter in Access Plaza (6:30 am–10 pm). The SoftBank counter on the Terminal 1 arrivals floor is open 7:30 am–9 pm. Or have the router sent to your hotel.

The Centrair post office: weekdays only

Centrair’s official page says Japan Post’s Tokoname Centrair branch is on the first floor of Terminal 1. Postal service runs on weekdays from 9:00 to 17:00, with no handling on Saturdays, Sundays, or national holidays. Sakura Mobile’s pickup page names the same office as its Centrair pickup point (“Tokoname Post Office Centrair Branch, Welcome Garden 1F”), with the same weekday hours. Japan Wireless also lists “Chubu Centrair: Post Office” as its airport pickup and needs the order one day ahead, excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays.

So a Friday-evening or Saturday arrival cannot collect a post-office order until Monday morning. For those flights, choose hotel delivery, an eSIM, or a counter with longer hours.

Sakura Mobile

Sakura Mobile is a Japan-based company selling travel SIM cards, eSIMs, and pocket Wi-Fi rentals. On 25 November 2026 its home page listed “from” prices of 1,375 yen for the unlimited travel eSIM, 3,850 yen for the travel SIM card, and 329 yen a day for pocket Wi-Fi, all tax included. The exact price depends on your dates. Its price page notes that 3 GB-a-day plans slow down after 3 GB in a day.

At Centrair, orders go to the airport post office and follow its weekday hours. Sakura Mobile also ships to any hotel in Japan. You throw the SIM card away after use. The pocket Wi-Fi goes back in the prepaid envelope it came with, dropped into any Japan Post postbox. At Centrair, Sakura Mobile places the postbox on the Terminal 1 departure lobby (3F), near check-in counter G. Drop it before security, because there are no postboxes airside.

Its Travel Voice + Data eSIM gives you a Japanese phone number. It needs in-person ID verification at select airport counters or the Shinjuku office, and the Centrair post office is a pickup-only counter. Confirm with Sakura Mobile before you order that plan for a Centrair arrival.

Japan Wireless

Japan Wireless rents pocket Wi-Fi and sells SIMs and eSIMs. On 25 November 2026 its home page showed short-term “truly unlimited” pocket Wi-Fi from US$4.68 a day. For the Chubu (Nagoya area) region it lists Centrair post office pickup, which needs the order one day ahead, excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays. It also lists delivery to hotels, post offices, and residences, with one day’s lead time. Returns go in the envelope to any postbox or post office in Japan.

NINJA WiFi (via Klook)

NINJA WiFi’s own Centrair page puts its counter at Terminal 1, 2F Access Plaza (“Global WiFi x NINJA WiFi”), open 6:30 am to 10 pm. That is the most useful set of hours here for evening arrivals. Its FAQ promises next-day pickup for orders placed by 16:00 the day before, but only at the airports it names, and Centrair is not one of them. Book a few days early. Klook sells an airport-pickup NINJA WiFi plan. Check that Chubu Centrair appears as a pickup location for your date. Also read the fair-use note and the loss fee on that listing.

Or skip pickup: eSIM

With an unlocked, eSIM-capable phone, an eSIM is the simplest choice for a Centrair arrival: install it at home, then switch it on when you land. Frequently asked questions

Can I pick up a pocket Wi-Fi at Chubu Centrair Airport on a weekend?
Not from the airport post office. Centrair says it handles mail on weekdays from 9:00 to 17:00 only, and Sakura Mobile and Japan Wireless both use it for Centrair pickup. For a weekend arrival, book a counter with longer hours (NINJA WiFi’s Access Plaza counter lists 6:30–22:00), choose hotel delivery, or use an eSIM.
Where is the post office at Centrair?
Japan Post’s Tokoname Centrair branch is on the first floor of Terminal 1. Sakura Mobile describes it as the Welcome Garden, 1F. It is open on weekdays from 9:00 to 17:00 and closed on Saturdays, Sundays, and national holidays (checked 25 November 2026).
How do I return a rented pocket Wi-Fi at Centrair?
For Sakura Mobile and Japan Wireless, put the router in the prepaid return envelope and drop it into a Japan Post postbox. Centrair lists postboxes on the Terminal 1 departures floor (3F), the Terminal 2 departures floor (2F), and in Access Plaza. Drop it before security, because there are no postboxes airside.
Can I buy a SIM card at Centrair without booking?
Yes. Centrair lists a 24-hour SIM card vending machine in front of the international arrivals gate and the SoftBank Global Rental Counter on the Terminal 1 arrivals floor (7:30–21:00). Check prices and plans there.
