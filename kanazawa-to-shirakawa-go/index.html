Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go

Official highway-bus fares and reservations. Go one way; I do not recommend a same-day return.

The usual way from Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go is the reserved highway bus on the Nohi / Hokutetsu Shirakawa-go line. This page is about how to go, and how to continue to Takayama if that is your next stop. It is not a plan for going to the village and back to Kanazawa in the same day. I do not recommend that sprint.

Official fares (adult, one way)

Figures from Nohi’s official English fare-revision notice, already cited on the Takayama to Shirakawa-go bus guide and rechecked against that official fare list. Recheck before you travel; highway-bus fares on this loop change on 1 December 2026.

Nohi’s official English page states that the Takayama–Shirakawa-go ride takes about 50 minutes. I am not inventing a running time for the Kanazawa–Shirakawa-go leg; use the official timetable for your departure. Delays of two hours or more can occur because of traffic, according to the same official page.

How to book

  1. Read the official Nohi English Shirakawa-go line page for the timetable, the reservation mark, luggage notes, and delay warnings.
  2. Book reservation-only services in advance on Japan Bus Online, or by phoning the Nohi reservation centre as described on the official page. Recheck the mark for your departure, including after the 1 December 2026 timetable change.
  3. From 1 December 2026, Nohi adds a Takayama–Kanazawa direct bus that does not stop at Shirakawa-go. Confirm that your booking is the Shirakawa-go line, not that direct service, if the village is your destination.

If you hold a Takayama–Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass, official pass conditions say the relevant Nohi / Hokutetsu buses on the Shirakawa-go line are covered, with some exceptions, and that reservations are required. See the pass guide and the official seller page.

At the village, then onward

Follow the official Shirakawa-go visitor rules: there is a fire ban, no rubbish bins for visitors, no night sightseeing for day visitors, and no drones. The official English site lists Seseragi Parking hours and fees if you arrive by car; most visitors on this loop arrive by bus. An observatory shuttle is listed at 300 yen on that official manners page — recheck it if you plan to go up.

If you continue to Takayama after the village, the official Nohi page says that leg is about 50 minutes. Stay overnight in Takayama rather than trying to return to Kanazawa the same evening. The Takayama 2-day itinerary picks up from there.

Why I do not recommend a same-day return

The buses exist in both directions, but a short stop between two longer winter or peak-season journeys leaves little time in the village and is a poor fit with the official request that day visitors not treat the settlement as a brief photo stop after dark. Go one way, or stay overnight nearby, and keep Kanazawa as its own day or two.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the bus from Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go?
Nohi’s official English page states that Takayama–Shirakawa-go takes about 50 minutes. It does not, in the text I checked, give a single running time for the Kanazawa leg. Use the official timetable for your departure, and allow for delays of two hours or more when traffic is heavy.
How much is the bus?
Nohi’s official fare-revision notice lists the adult one-way fare between Kanazawa and Shirakawa-go as 2,800 yen through 30 November 2026 and 2,900 yen from 1 December 2026. Recheck before you travel.
Can I go and return to Kanazawa the same day?
The buses run in both directions, but I do not recommend a same-day return. Go one way toward Takayama, or give the village and Kanazawa separate days.
Could I book the wrong bus after 1 December 2026?
Yes. From 1 December 2026 Nohi adds a Takayama–Kanazawa direct bus that does not stop at Shirakawa-go. Confirm that your booking is the Shirakawa-go line.
