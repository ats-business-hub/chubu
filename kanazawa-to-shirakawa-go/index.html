Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go: when winter buses are full

The usual way from Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go is the reserved highway bus on the Nohi / Hokutetsu Shirakawa-go line. This page is about how to go, and how to continue to Takayama if that is your next stop. It is not a plan for going to the village and back to Kanazawa in the same day. I do not recommend that sprint.

Nohi’s official English page states that the Takayama–Shirakawa-go ride takes about 50 minutes. I am not inventing a running time for the Kanazawa–Shirakawa-go leg; use the official timetable for your departure. Delays of two hours or more can occur because of traffic, according to the same official page.

How to book

  1. Read the official Nohi English Shirakawa-go line page for the timetable, the reservation mark, luggage notes, and delay warnings. Many Kanazawa–Shirakawa-go trips are reservation-only.
  2. Book reservation-only services in advance on Japan Bus Online, or by phone. The English Nohi page lists the Hokurikutetsudou (Hokutetsu) reservation centre for the Kanazawa line as 9:00am–6:00pm, TEL 076-234-0123. Hokutetsu’s own highway-bus page for this corridor, checked the same day, lists the telephone service centre as 8:00–18:00, year-round, and says the route is reserved seating, tickets must be bought by the stated deadline or the reservation is cancelled, and reservations and sales start one month before the travel date. Follow the page you book through, or call to confirm.
  3. From 1 December 2026, Nohi adds a Takayama–Kanazawa direct bus that does not stop at Shirakawa-go. Confirm that your booking is the Shirakawa-go line, not that direct service, if the village is your destination.

If you hold a Takayama–Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass, official pass conditions say the relevant Nohi / Hokutetsu buses on the Shirakawa-go line are covered, with some exceptions, and that reservations are required. The official seller page, rechecked 26 November 2026, still lists the adult pass at 19,800 yen and the child pass at 9,900 yen for five consecutive days. See the pass guide and the official seller page.

The official 1 December 2026 Nohi timetable PDF says the line uses the weekend and holiday timetable from 29 December to 3 January (and 13–15 August), that some trips do not run on those dates, and that Gokayama Suginami is served only from 1 April to 30 November. Do not plan a winter stop at Suginami from this bus. Recheck the official timetable for your date; this page does not reproduce every departure.

When the Kanazawa bus is full

On peak winter weekends and around the light-up period, reserved Kanazawa–Shirakawa-go seats can sell out. This is a reading of official reservation rules and timetables, not a report of a trip I have taken.

  1. Look at another official departure the same day on Japan Bus Online or by phone. Hokutetsu says a reservation is cancelled automatically if you do not buy the ticket by the stated deadline. That is a reason to book and pay early, not a promise that a seat will reappear later.
  2. Use the Takayama corridor instead if seats remain there. The official ride between Takayama and Shirakawa-go is about 50 minutes. See Takayama to Shirakawa-go bus: winter day trip and booking. Reaching Takayama first (rail or the Nagoya–Takayama highway bus) is a different booking.
  3. Do not book the new Takayama–Kanazawa direct bus expecting a village stop. From 1 December 2026 it does not stop at Shirakawa-go.
  4. Stay overnight in or near the village if lodging is available. The village says night sightseeing is for overnight guests only. Keep Kanazawa as its own day rather than forcing a same-day return.
  5. If you can start from Nagoya, see Shirakawa-go day trip from Nagoya, including a guided tour option.

Japan Bus Tickets does not sell the Shirakawa-go village bus. It does sell reserved Nagoya–Kanazawa and Nagoya–Takayama highway buses, which only help if you still need to reach those cities first. Official winter clothing advice is in What to wear in Shirakawa-go and Takayama in winter.

At the village, then onward

Follow the official Shirakawa-go visitor rules: there is a fire ban, no rubbish bins for visitors, no night sightseeing for day visitors, and no drones. The official English site lists Seseragi Parking hours and fees if you arrive by car; most visitors on this loop arrive by bus. An observatory shuttle is listed at 300 yen on that official manners page — recheck it if you plan to go up.

If you continue to Takayama after the village, the official Nohi page says that leg is about 50 minutes. Stay overnight in Takayama rather than trying to return to Kanazawa the same evening. The Takayama 2-day itinerary picks up from there.

Why I do not recommend a same-day return

The buses exist in both directions, but a short stop between two longer winter or peak-season journeys leaves little time in the village and is a poor fit with the official request that day visitors not treat the settlement as a brief photo stop after dark. Go one way, or stay overnight nearby, and keep Kanazawa as its own day or two.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the bus from Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go?
Nohi’s official English page states that Takayama–Shirakawa-go takes about 50 minutes. It does not, in the text I checked, give a single running time for the Kanazawa leg. Use the official timetable for your departure, and allow for delays of two hours or more when traffic is heavy.
How much is the bus?
Nohi’s official fare-revision notice lists the adult one-way fare between Kanazawa and Shirakawa-go as 2,800 yen through 30 November 2026 and 2,900 yen from 1 December 2026. Recheck before you travel.
Can I go and return to Kanazawa the same day?
The buses run in both directions, but I do not recommend a same-day return. Go one way toward Takayama, or give the village and Kanazawa separate days.
Could I book the wrong bus after 1 December 2026?
Yes. From 1 December 2026 Nohi adds a Takayama–Kanazawa direct bus that does not stop at Shirakawa-go. Confirm that your booking is the Shirakawa-go line.
What if the Kanazawa–Shirakawa-go bus is full?
Try another official departure, or the Takayama corridor if seats remain there. Japan Bus Tickets does not sell the village bus. A same-day Kanazawa return is still a poor fit with village manners.
Does the bus stop at Gokayama in winter?
Nohi’s official 1 December 2026 timetable PDF says Gokayama Suginami is served only from 1 April to 30 November. Do not plan a winter stop there from this bus.
