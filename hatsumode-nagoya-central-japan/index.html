Hatsumode 2027 in Nagoya and Central Japan: access, overnight trains and New Year hours

Atsuta Jingu, Osu Kannon, Toyokawa Inari and Ise Jingu, plus the Nagoya subway overnight service and Meitetsu and JR holiday timetables, from official pages checked 27 November 2026. Not a hands-on visit report.

Hatsumode is the first shrine or temple visit of the new year. Around Nagoya, the main options are Atsuta Jingu in the city's south, Osu Kannon near Sakae, Toyokawa Inari further east in Aichi, and, for a full day out, Ise Jingu in Mie. This guide covers access and what the official sources have confirmed for New Year 2027 (Reiwa 9). It covers what to know before you go, not the history of each site. Official pages were checked on 27 November 2026. Where an official page had not yet published something for 2027, this guide says so instead of using last year's schedule. It is researched from those pages, not a visit report.

Hatsumode 2027 at a glance

  • New Year's Eve is Thursday 31 December 2026. New Year's Day is Friday 1 January 2027, a public holiday (city.nagoya.jp).
  • Nagoya subway runs overnight on most lines from after the last train on 31 December until the first train on 1 January (city.nagoya.jp).
  • Meitetsu runs its weekend/holiday timetable from Tuesday 29 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027. Its notices checked on 27 November 2026 did not announce overnight service on New Year's Eve (meitetsu.co.jp; meitetsu.co.jp).
  • JR Central runs extra winter trains. Nozomi and the Hida, Shinano and Nanki limited expresses are all-reserved over year-end and New Year (jr-central.co.jp).

Atsuta Jingu: access by gate

Atsuta Jingu is at 1-1-1 Jingu, Atsuta-ku, Nagoya (nagoya-info.jp; aichinow.pref.aichi.jp). The grounds have several gates, and choosing the right station can save you time at New Year:

  • South (main) gate: Atsuta-jingu-denmacho Station on the Meijo Line, Exit 1, about 7 minutes' walk (nagoya-info.jp).
  • West gate: Atsuta-jingu-nishi Station on the Meijo Line, Exit 2, about 7 minutes' walk (nagoya-info.jp).
  • East gate: Jingu-mae Station on the Meitetsu Nagoya Line, about 3 minutes' walk, or JR Atsuta Station on the Tokaido Line, about 8 minutes' walk (nagoya-info.jp; aichinow.pref.aichi.jp).

Hours: The shrine's official website (atsutajingu.or.jp) returned an error when we checked on 27 November 2026, so this guide gives no New Year 2027 gate, prayer or crowd-control times. Nagoya's official tourism page lists no set opening hours for the shrine grounds (nagoya-info.jp), but New Year arrangements can differ. Check the shrine's official site before you go.

Getting there on New Year's Eve: Both Meijo Line stations are on the Meijo Line loop, which is scheduled to run overnight (city.nagoya.jp). Meitetsu and JR had not announced overnight New Year's Eve service in the notices checked on 27 November 2026, so the Meijo Line is the safer plan late at night.

Osu Kannon: the midnight bell and the first three days

Osu Kannon is in Naka-ku, Nagoya. Take the Tsurumai Line to Osu Kannon Station; the temple is just outside Exit 2. It is about 2.5 km from Nagoya Station. City buses also stop at "Osu Kannon" from Nagoya Station, Sakae and Kanayama. The temple has no parking (osu-kannon.jp).

The temple's annual-events page describes its usual New Year in general terms, not specific to 2027 (osu-kannon.jp):

  • 31 December: a repentance service from 19:00, with the bell sounding at midnight as the year turns.
  • 1–3 January: New Year visits begin at midnight, with a morning fire ritual (goma) held alongside the bell. The page says more than 500,000 people visit over the first three days.
  • Bell-ringing ticket: 1,000 yen.
  • New Year fire-prayer services: 1–3 January, 10:00–17:00.

The page describes the usual pattern each year, not a 2027 schedule, so check it again closer to the date.

Overnight access: The Tsurumai Line's overnight service runs from Kami-Otai to Akaike, which includes Osu Kannon Station. On that night it will not run through onto the Meitetsu Inuyama or Toyota lines (city.nagoya.jp).

Toyokawa Inari: a longer trip east on Meitetsu or JR

Toyokawa Inari is at 1 Toyokawa-cho, Toyokawa, Aichi. It is about a 5-minute walk from both JR Toyokawa Station on the Iida Line and Toyokawa-inari Station on the Meitetsu Toyokawa Line. From Nagoya, the temple suggests the Meitetsu Main Line, riding an express bound for Toyokawa-inari (toyokawainari.jp).

The temple's events calendar lists the hatsumode period as 1–15 January, with a blessing chant service on 1 January. The page calls these dates a guide to the usual schedule each year, so check the page again for 2027 details (toyokawainari.jp). Parking is limited and the temple recommends public transport. Standard cars pay 600 yen at the large car park (toyokawainari.jp). The events page notes that traffic restrictions may apply on surrounding roads at busy times such as hatsumode (toyokawainari.jp). The temple's phone number is 0533-85-2030, 9:00–17:00 (toyokawainari.jp).

The long hatsumode period is useful if you want to avoid the first three days. Meitetsu's weekend timetable applies from 29 December to 3 January (meitetsu.co.jp), so check departures on that timetable.

Ise Jingu as a regional New Year option

Ise Jingu is a full-day trip from Nagoya, with Geku (the Outer Shrine) and Naiku (the Inner Shrine) as separate sites. For routes and planning, see Ise Jingu day trip from Nagoya. What matters for New Year:

  • Usual hours: 5:00–18:00 in January and 5:00–17:00 in December (isejingu.or.jp).
  • New Year hours change. The shrine says its hours differ over New Year and points visitors to its latest-information page (isejingu.or.jp). Its latest-information page had not posted New Year 2027 hours when we checked on 27 November 2026 (isejingu.or.jp).
  • New Year's Eve: From 31 December into 1 January, kagura (sacred dance) is performed through the night at the Kaguraden of both shrines, and charms and amulets are offered (isejingu.or.jp).
  • Ceremonies are not opening hours. The Saitansai on 1 January 2027 is listed at 4:00 at Geku and 7:00 at Naiku (isejingu.or.jp). These are ceremony times, not gate-opening times. Some ceremonies held during visiting hours can be viewed from the approach path (isejingu.or.jp).
  • Order of visit: There is no fixed rule, but the long-standing custom is Geku first, then Naiku (isejingu.or.jp).

Nagoya subway overnight service on New Year's Eve

The City of Nagoya announced on 19 October 2026 that the subway will run overnight from after the last train on 31 December 2026 until the first train on 1 January 2027, for people making New Year visits (city.nagoya.jp):

Line Overnight section
Higashiyama Takabata–Fujigaoka, about every 30 minutes
Meijo Loop service, about every 30 minutes
Meiko Kanayama–Nagoyako, about every 30 minutes
Tsurumai Kami-Otai–Akaike, about every 30 minutes
Sakura-dori Taiko-dori–Tokushige, about every 30 minutes
Kamiiida Not listed in the announcement

Some station exits will be partly closed overnight. Detailed timetables are due on the Nagoya City Transportation Bureau website on 1 December (city.nagoya.jp; bureau site: kotsu.city.nagoya.jp). Check those timetables for departure times before you set out. The announcement does not cover city buses.

Meitetsu and JR Central over the holidays

Meitetsu: - From 29 December 2026 to 3 January 2027, Meitetsu runs its weekend/holiday timetable. The notice was posted on 18 November 2026 (meitetsu.co.jp). - During that period, the "なごや特割２土休日" (Nagoya Tokuwari 2, weekend/holiday version) discount ticket can be used. - μ-ticket (Myu Ticket) reserved-seat tickets bought through the ticketless service are charged at the off-peak web discount rate, except for trips to or from Chubu Centrair International Airport Station. - On 29 December, two rapid limited express trains arrive at Toyohashi a little later: 8:07 becomes 8:09, and 8:37 becomes 8:42. The website's timetable search will still show the old times for that day (all from meitetsu.co.jp).

This notice changes which timetable runs. It does not announce overnight service on New Year's Eve, and its news list showed no overnight-service notice when checked on 27 November 2026 (meitetsu.co.jp).

JR Central: The winter extra-train release of 22 October 2026 covers 1 December 2026 to 28 February 2027 (jr-central.co.jp): - 25 December to 5 January is treated as the year-end/New Year period. - About 449 Shinkansen trains a day are planned in that period. The release notes the figures may change. - Nozomi is all-reserved over year-end/New Year and the Coming-of-Age Day long weekend. - The Shinano, Hida and Nanki limited expresses are all-reserved from 25 December to 5 January.

The release does not mention overnight service on New Year's Eve. If you plan to use JR late that night, for example to Atsuta Station, check JR Central's announcements nearer the date.

Timing and crowds

The official pages give one firm crowd figure: Osu Kannon says more than 500,000 people visit over the first three days of the year, as a recurring description (osu-kannon.jp). No official crowd data was found for Atsuta Jingu, Toyokawa Inari or Ise for 2027.

Practical points from the sources: - Toyokawa Inari's hatsumode period usually runs to 15 January (toyokawainari.jp), so a visit after the first few days still counts. - Ise's ceremony times are not opening times. Wait for the shrine's New Year notice before planning a very early arrival (isejingu.or.jp). - Seats on Nozomi and the Hida/Shinano/Nanki limited expresses can't be bought on the spot as unreserved over 25 December–5 January, so book ahead (jr-central.co.jp). - Large bags make crowded shrine approaches harder. Frequently asked questions

Is Atsuta Jingu open all night on New Year's Eve?
This guide can't confirm it. The shrine's official site returned an error when we checked on 27 November 2026, and Nagoya's tourism page lists no set hours for the shrine grounds (nagoya-info.jp).
Does the Nagoya subway run all night on 31 December 2026?
Yes on most lines, from after the last train on 31 December until the first train on 1 January. The Kamiiida Line is not in the announced list. Timetables are due on 1 December (city.nagoya.jp).
Does Meitetsu run overnight on New Year's Eve?
No overnight service had been announced when we checked on 27 November 2026. Meitetsu has announced weekend/holiday timetables from 29 December to 3 January (meitetsu.co.jp).
What time does Ise Jingu open on 1 January 2027?
The shrine had not posted it when we checked on 27 November 2026. It says New Year hours differ from its usual 5:00 opening (isejingu.or.jp). The 4:00 and 7:00 Saitansai times are ceremonies, not gate hours (isejingu.or.jp).
How do I get to Osu Kannon?
Take the Tsurumai Line to Osu Kannon Station; the temple is just outside Exit 2 (osu-kannon.jp).
