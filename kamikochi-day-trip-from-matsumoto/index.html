Kamikochi day trip from Matsumoto

Kamikochi is closed for this season. The official website says the 2026 season ran through 15 November, that bus services and facilities closed from 16 November, and that the area is scheduled to reopen on 17 April 2027 (Grand Open 27 April). Official Japanese notices describe the winter closure as 16 November 2026 through 16 April 2027. Regular buses do not run into the valley in winter. This page is a planning guide for the next open season — not a reason to go now.

Can you do it as a day trip?

Yes, in season. The official Kamikochi site says the area is “easily done in a day trip from Matsumoto and Hida Takayama.” Private cars and motorcycles are forbidden from entering the valley all year. You go by reserved bus (or by parking at Sawando or Hirayu and taking a shuttle or taxi).

The official FAQ says the last bus leaves Kamikochi Bus Terminal around 6 p.m., and that you should double-check. There are no ATMs. A baggage room at the bus terminal (not coin lockers) is listed at 350–500 yen a day, open 6:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Stay on the path; do not collect plants, feed animals, litter, bring pets, ride bicycles on the trails, or fly drones.

Official access from Matsumoto

Alpico’s official Matsumoto–Kamikochi page is the booking source. As of 22 November 2026 it still publishes the 2026 season (17 April–15 November 2026). The 2027 timetable and fares had not been posted yet. Treat the figures below as last season’s published fares and recheck them on Alpico before you pay.

  • From 2025, all buses between Matsumoto and Kamikochi require reservations for both the outbound and the return. Book about one month ahead on the reservation links from Alpico’s page (Japan Bus Online or highwaybus.com). Same-day tickets may be sold if seats remain, but buses fill up.
  • Two direct buses, the National Park Liner, run from Matsumoto Bus Terminal to Kamikochi (in the 2026 timetable, 5:30 a.m. and 10:15 a.m.). Alpico says they are usually fully booked, and that there is no direct bus for the return. 2026 one-way adult fares: 5,000 yen (5:30 departure) and 4,600 yen (10:15 departure).
  • The usual route is the Alpico Kamikochi Line train from Matsumoto to Shin-Shimashima, then a reserved bus to Kamikochi. The 2026 train fare published by Alpico is 710 yen adult one way, sold at vending machines at JR Matsumoto Station, cash only — IC cards and credit cards are not accepted. Train seats are not reserved. The 2026 Shin-Shimashima–Kamikochi bus fare is 3,100 yen adult one way.
  • Only one-way tickets are sold; Alpico says there is no round-trip fare or round-trip discount.

If you select “Matsumoto Station” as the drop-off on an online return reservation, Alpico says the ticket includes both the bus and the train fare.

If you come by car

Park at Sawando (closer to Matsumoto) or Hirayu (closer to Takayama) and take a shuttle bus or taxi. The official “By Car” page says parking typically costs 700 yen and 600 yen per day — recheck on the day. Published taxi examples on the same page include Sawando to Kamikochi Bus Terminal 5,200 yen, and Hirayu Onsen Area to Kamikochi Bus Terminal 6,000 yen.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kamikochi open now?
No. The official website says the 2026 season ran through 15 November, that buses and facilities closed from 16 November, and that the area is scheduled to reopen on 17 April 2027 (Grand Open 27 April). Regular buses do not run into the valley in winter.
Can I drive into Kamikochi?
No. The official By Car page says private cars and motorcycles are prohibited from entering all year. Park at Sawando or Hirayu and take a shuttle bus or taxi.
Do I need a bus reservation?
Alpico’s official page says that from 2025, all buses between Matsumoto and Kamikochi require reservations in both directions. Book from the official Alpico links. The 2027 timetable and fares had not been published as of 22 November 2026, so recheck before you pay.
