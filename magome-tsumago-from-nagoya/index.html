Magome to Tsumago from Nagoya: the Nakasendo walk

An 8 km walk between two post towns, as a day trip from Nagoya. Official buses, cash fares, and luggage forwarding.

Some links on this page are affiliate links for Radical Storage; if you book through them I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. I created the paid guide mentioned here. Official pages remain the primary place to check buses and services. #ad

The Magome–Tsumago walk follows a preserved stretch of the old Nakasendo road between two post towns. From Nagoya it works as a full day trip. Fares and services below come from Nakatsugawa City, the Nagiso Tourism Association bus sheet dated 1 April 2026, and the official Tsumago site, checked 25 November 2026. They are in Japanese yen.

Which direction?

Start in Magome and finish in Tsumago if you can. The Kiso Ontake tourism office says that direction involves less uphill walking. It also means you arrive at Nagiso Station on the JR line for the trip back. Bring water and snacks before you set off.

Luggage forwarding: the season ends 30 November

  • Regular service (20 March – 30 November 2026): 1,000 yen per item. Drop off 8:30–11:30 at the Magome or Tsumago tourist information centers (or Magome-juku BASE / Otsumago inns), and collect 13:00–17:00 at the other end. Same-day delivery only.
  • Winter service (1 December – 19 March): the Tsumago site’s 19 November 2026 notice lists 2,500 yen per item from December 2026, drop-off until 15:00 and collection by 16:30. Closed 29 December – 3 January.
  • The Tsumago Tourist Information Center also rents bear bells for the trail, per the Nagiso Tourism Association.

Is it covered by a rail pass?

The nationwide Japan Rail Pass covers JR trains such as the Shinano; the local buses to Magome and from Tsumago are not JR. The Takayama–Hokuriku Area Pass does not include this route. See JR Pass vs regional passes.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Magome to Tsumago walk?
About 8 km, taking 3–4 hours, according to Nakatsugawa City’s official leaflet.
How do I get from Nagoya to Magome?
Take JR to Nakatsugawa (about 50 minutes by Limited Express Shinano, or 80 minutes by local train), then the Kita-Ena Kotsu bus from stand No. 3 to Magome, about 25 minutes. The bus fare is 800 yen, cash, on the April 2026 official timetable.
Is the Magome–Tsumago luggage service available in winter?
Yes, as a winter service from 1 December to 19 March. The official Tsumago notice lists 2,500 yen per item from December 2026, with drop-off until 15:00. The regular 1,000-yen service ends on 30 November.
