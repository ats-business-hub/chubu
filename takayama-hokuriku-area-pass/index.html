Takayama–Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass

Official five-day price and coverage for the Takayama, Shirakawa-go, and Kanazawa loop. Not the nationwide Japan Rail Pass.

The Takayama–Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass is a five-consecutive-day pass for the official Central Japan loop that includes Takayama, Shirakawa-go, Kanazawa, and parts of the Hokuriku Shinkansen. It is not the nationwide Japan Rail Pass, and it is usually the better first comparison for this circuit. Official conditions and prices below are from the official seller page, checked 22 November 2026. Recheck before you buy; pass rules change.

Buy from the official seller, touristpass.jp, or via JR-WEST ONLINE TRAIN RESERVATION. Official sales open one month ahead, from 10:00 Japan time on the same day of the preceding month. Recheck that booking window on the official page.

What it covers

  • Unlimited travel on the JR local and limited-express trains listed on the official map, plus the Hokuriku Shinkansen between Toyama and Tsuruga.
  • Reserved seats on those JR limited expresses and on that Shinkansen segment are free up to six times. After that, you pay the reserved-seat fee.
  • From 25 December 2026 through 5 January 2027, all seats on the Limited Express Hida are reserved. Official conditions say you must get a free reserved-seat ticket before boarding during that period.
  • Nohi, Hokutetsu, Meitetsu, and Toyama Chitetsu buses on the Shirakawa-go / Kanazawa and Takayama / Toyama lines between Takayama Nohi Bus Center, Shirakawa-go, and Kanazawa or Toyama, with some exceptions. Reservations are required for those buses.

What it does not cover

  • The Tokaido Shinkansen between Kyoto and Shin-Osaka is not covered.
  • The fare between Toyama and Unazuki is not included.
  • It is not a nationwide Japan Rail Pass. If your trip is mostly this loop, compare this area pass first rather than defaulting to the national pass.

Coverage is specific. Use the official route map on touristpass.jp for your exact trains, and recheck exceptions before you count on a bus or a branch line.

When it is usually worth comparing

The pass can make sense if, inside five consecutive days, you combine a long rail ride into the area (for example the Limited Express Hida to Takayama, or the Hokuriku Shinkansen into Kanazawa’s side of the map) with the reserved Shirakawa-go highway buses. If you only need one short train and no buses, ordinary tickets may be cheaper — I am not inventing a break-even total; add the official fares for your own dates.

For planning the days the pass would cover, see Takayama 2-day, Kanazawa 2-day, and the bus notes for Takayama and Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the Takayama–Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass?
The official seller page, checked 22 November 2026, lists 19,800 yen for adults and 9,900 yen for children aged 6–11, valid for five consecutive days. Recheck before you buy.
Is this the nationwide Japan Rail Pass?
No. It is a regional pass for listed trains and some highway buses on the Takayama–Hokuriku circuit. The Tokaido Shinkansen between Kyoto and Shin-Osaka is not covered.
Do I still need a bus reservation if I have the pass?
Yes. Official pass conditions say reservations are required for the listed Nohi, Hokutetsu, Meitetsu, and Toyama Chitetsu buses on the Shirakawa-go and related lines, with some exceptions. Book on Japan Bus Online or as the official bus page instructs.
What happens on the Limited Express Hida at year-end?
Official conditions say that from 25 December 2026 through 5 January 2027, all Hida seats are reserved, and you must get a free reserved-seat ticket before boarding.
