2026 season and 2027 reopening

Official Kamikochi pages described the 2026 season as open from 17 April (with a later “grand open”) through 15 November. A closing ceremony was held on 15 November 2026, and winter closure began on 16 November 2026. Nohi’s official Kamikochi-line notes, checked on 22 November 2026, say 2026 service on that line has ended.

The official homepage now states that Kamikochi is closed for this season and “will reopen next season on April 17.” Treat mid-April to mid-November as the usual window, and recheck 2027 bus dates, combination tickets, and facility openings on the official Kamikochi and Nohi sites before you book. Not every facility opens on the first bus day.

How the Takayama approach works (when buses run)

From Takayama, the usual official pattern is a Nohi bus toward Hirayu, then a connecting bus from Hirayu to Kamikochi. Private cars are not allowed into the Kamikochi valley; you finish the trip on the official bus (or an approved taxi service as described on official pages). I am not copying a standing timetable here: Nohi’s official combination-ticket page has used a sample early departure from Takayama and a connecting departure from Hirayu as an example itinerary, not as “the” times for every day.

For the 2026 season, Nohi’s official combination-ticket (“value kippu”) page listed a 2-day ticket from Takayama at 10,500 yen for adults and 5,250 yen for children in elementary school, and a 3-day ticket at 11,000 / 5,500 yen. Those tickets included a 2- or 3-day free ride between Takayama and Shinhotaka, one round trip on the Shinhotaka Ropeway, and one round trip between Hirayu and Kamikochi, with a change at Hirayu. Those were 2026 season products. Do not treat those yen figures as current 2027 fares; recheck the official Nohi page when the next season is announced. I am not quoting third-party one-way connecting fares that I have not found on an official Nohi fare table.

A realistic day in the valley

When the park is open, a day trip from Takayama is long. Official Kamikochi pages highlight Kappa Bridge, Taisho Pond, and walking the maintained paths. Stay on the path. Official rules also say: no collecting plants or creatures, no feeding animals, no littering (take your rubbish with you), no pets or other non-native species, no bicycles on the trails, and no drones.

The official FAQ says the last bus leaves Kamikochi Bus Terminal around 6 p.m., but you should double-check the live timetable. There are no ATMs in the valley. A baggage room on the first floor of the bus terminal is listed (tel: 0263-95-2034) at 350 yen small, 400 yen medium, and 500 yen large (600 yen for oversize), open 6:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., with no coin lockers. Day visitors can use some hotel-lobby shops and baths; recheck each facility.

If you are in Takayama while Kamikochi is closed

Do not plan a Kamikochi day from mid-November through mid-April. Use the Takayama 2-day itinerary or the winter notes instead, and keep this page for when official 2027 dates are confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kamikochi open now?
No. As of 22 November 2026 the official Kamikochi website says the area is closed for this season and will reopen next season on 17 April. The 2026 season ran through 15 November.
How do I get there from Takayama when it is open?
The usual official pattern is a Nohi bus to Hirayu and a connecting bus into Kamikochi. Private cars are not allowed in the valley. Recheck the live Nohi timetable and any combination ticket for the season you are travelling.
What did the 2026 combination ticket cost?
Nohi’s official 2026 combination-ticket page listed 10,500 yen for a 2-day adult ticket from Takayama (5,250 yen for elementary-school children) and 11,000 / 5,500 yen for 3 days. Those figures were for the 2026 season. Recheck 2027 prices on the official Nohi page.
What time is the last bus out?
The official FAQ says the last bus leaves Kamikochi Bus Terminal around 6 p.m., and tells you to double-check. Do not treat that as a fixed year-round time.
