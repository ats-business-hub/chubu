Inuyama Castle: the easiest half day

Inuyama Castle’s official English site lists opening hours of 9:00–17:00 (last entry 16:30), closed 29–31 December, and admission of 1,000 yen for adults and 200 yen for children under 14. IC transport cards and several e-money brands are accepted at the Front Gate ticket office. Its access page gives about 25 minutes from Meitetsu Nagoya to Inuyama Station on a Rapid Limited Express or Limited Express, then about a 20-minute walk. The Japanese official site also posts a live waiting-time estimate for entering the keep, and says entry can be restricted when crowded.

Magome–Tsumago: the best walking day

The Nakasendo walk between two restored post towns is about 8 km and 3–4 hours, according to Nakatsugawa City. The regular luggage-forwarding service between the towns ends on 30 November 2026; from 1 December a winter service runs at a higher price. Details and bus fares: Magome–Tsumago from Nagoya.

Takayama and Shirakawa-go: possible, but long

Both are doable from Nagoya in a day, but most of the day is transport. If you can, stay one night in Takayama — see the Takayama 2-day itinerary. Before you buy tickets leg by leg, check whether the Takayama–Hokuriku Area Pass covers your route.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest day trip from Nagoya?
Inuyama Castle. Its official access page gives about 25 minutes from Meitetsu Nagoya to Inuyama Station, then about a 20-minute walk. As of 25 November 2026 admission is 1,000 yen for adults, 9:00–17:00 (last entry 16:30).
Can I visit Takayama and Shirakawa-go as a day trip from Nagoya?
It is possible but long, because most of the day is transport. An overnight in Takayama is easier.
Is Magome–Tsumago a good day trip from Nagoya?
Yes, if you like walking. Nakatsugawa City describes an 8 km walk of 3–4 hours between the post towns, reached by JR to Nakatsugawa and a 25-minute bus to Magome.
