Shinhotaka Ropeway from Takayama in winter: fares, the Nohi bus, and what can stop you

Open year-round. Fares, bus fares, parking and weather warnings from the operators’ own pages. Not a hands-on test.

Shinhotaka Ropeway in Okuhida (Takayama City, Gifu) is the high-mountain trip you can still make from Takayama in winter. The operator lists it as open year-round, and Nohi Bus runs from Takayama via Hirayu Onsen to the lower station. This page collects the winter-relevant facts from the official pages, checked 26 November 2026. The ropeway’s first and last cars change by season and are published only as a timetable image, so they are not repeated here: open the operator’s Business Hours and Fares page for your date.

Does the ropeway run in winter?

Yes. The operator’s English and Japanese sites list it as open year-round, with the No. 1 Ropeway (Shinhotaka Onsen to Nabedaira-kogen) leaving every hour at :00 and :30 and the No. 2 Ropeway (Shirakaba-daira to Nishi-Hotaka-guchi) every hour at :15 and :45. First and last departures follow the seasonal timetable.

The operator also says service may be suspended for adverse weather or scheduled maintenance. It announced a full maintenance closure from Tuesday 24 to Friday 27 November 2026, and on 26 November the status panel on its home page showed both ropeways closed and −5°C. Nohi Bus’s route page describes the ropeway as running daily except in extreme weather or high winds. Neither gives a wind-speed threshold, so check the live status panel on shinhotaka-ropeway.jp on the morning you go. The operator also promotes a winter snowshoe tour, which Nohi notes needs a reservation.

  • Children: the child fare is for elementary school students. Junior high and older pay adult fares. Preschool children ride free with an adult.
  • Validity: uphill tickets are valid on the day of issue only; downhill tickets for three days including the day of issue.
  • Baggage: items over 6 kg need an excess baggage ticket (No. 1 + 2: 1,000 yen round trip, 500 yen one way). Skis, snowboards and ice axes without covered tips are not allowed on board.
  • Groups of 20 or more pay a group fare (adult round trip on No. 1 + 2: 3,500 yen).
  • Disability discount: 50% off with a valid disability handbook or Mirairo ID shown at the window; one caregiver may also get 50% off depending on the handbook type and grade.
  • Priority boarding is sold as a 300-yen add-on; it is not a ticket by itself.

Combination tickets

  • “Otoku-pon” set — 4,500 yen adult / 2,250 yen child: a return ropeway ticket plus 500-yen vouchers for food and shopping inside the ropeway (three for adults, two for children). The operator says this saves 800 yen (adult) and 650 yen (child). No change is given on vouchers.
  • “Okuhida” All-in-One Value Ticket: unlimited Nohi Bus rides on the Hirayu–Shinhotaka line plus one ropeway return (No. 1 and No. 2). 2-day from Takayama 7,700 yen (child 3,850); 2-day from Hirayu 5,400 yen (child 2,700); 3-day from Takayama 8,200 yen (child 4,100). Sold at Takayama Nohi Bus Center, Hirayu Bus Terminal and as a mobile ticket; not valid during ropeway maintenance periods.

From Takayama, compare the All-in-One with paying separately: 2,200 yen each way on the bus plus 3,800 yen for the ropeway round trip.

Getting there from Takayama by Nohi Bus

Nohi’s Hirayu–Shinhotaka line runs from Takayama Nohi Bus Center via Hirayu Onsen to the Shinhotaka Ropeway stop. Adult one-way fares to the ropeway (Nohi’s fare table): from Takayama 2,200 yen, Hirayu Onsen 910 yen, Fukuji Onsen 660 yen, Shinhirayu Onsen 570 yen, Tochio Onsen 490 yen. Children aged 6–12 pay half.

Buy tickets in advance at the window or ticket machine at Takayama Nohi Bus Center, Hirayu Onsen, Akandana Parking Area, Honoki-daira or the ropeway; if those are closed, pay on board. Nohi does not allow ski boots or spiked shoes on the bus.

Timetable: the timetable PDF linked from Nohi’s Japanese route page on 26 November 2026 is dated 1 April 2026. In it, return buses leave the ropeway roughly hourly, the last all-year departure is 17:55, and an 18:35 bus runs only from 1 April to 30 November. Nohi has announced timetable revisions on other routes from 1 December 2026, so open the current Shinhotaka line page before you travel rather than relying on these times. 29 December–3 January runs on the weekend and holiday timetable.

Snow delays: Nohi warns that buses may be significantly delayed, especially by heavy snow and by traffic in Takayama City, and that both arrivals and departures can be affected. Take a morning bus and leave a margin for the last return.

A simple winter day

  1. Check the ropeway status panel and Nohi’s current timetable the evening before and again in the morning.
  2. Take a morning Nohi bus from Takayama Nohi Bus Center toward Hirayu Onsen and Shinhotaka Ropeway.
  3. Ride No. 1 then No. 2 to Nishi-Hotaka-guchi; check the seasonal last cars down for your date.
  4. Come down with time to spare for a bus back. Dress for real cold: see What to wear in winter.

Shinhotaka is not Kamikochi. Both are near Hirayu, but Kamikochi is a valley with a seasonal road closure; see Kamikochi day trip from Takayama for the open season.

Frequently asked questions

Is Shinhotaka Ropeway open in winter?
Yes. The operator lists it as open year-round, with No. 1 departures at :00 and :30 and No. 2 at :15 and :45. Service may stop for bad weather or maintenance, including the full closure from 24 to 27 November 2026. First and last cars follow a seasonal timetable on the official site.
How much is the ropeway?
An adult round trip on No. 1 + No. 2 is 3,800 yen (child 1,900 yen); one way is 2,400 yen (child 1,200 yen), per the operator’s fare page checked 26 November 2026.
How much is the bus from Takayama?
Nohi Bus lists 2,200 yen one way for adults from Takayama Nohi Bus Center to Shinhotaka Ropeway, and 910 yen from Hirayu Onsen. Children aged 6–12 pay half.
Can I park at Nabedaira in winter?
No. Nabedaira Kogen Parking closes in the snow season, from 24 November 2026 this winter. Park at Shinhotaka Onsen Parking and board at Shinhotaka Onsen Station.
