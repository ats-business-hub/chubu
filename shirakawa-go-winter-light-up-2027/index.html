Shirakawa-go winter light-up 2027

Four reserved evenings only. No ticket on the day. Official pages first.

I created the paid guide mentioned here. Some links on this page are affiliate links for GetYourGuide, Klook and Viator; if you book through them I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Official operator pages remain the primary place to book. I do not recommend day-tripping Shirakawa-go from Kanazawa and back, and I do not treat the nationwide Japan Rail Pass as the default for this loop.

The 41st Shirakawa-go light-up is a limited, reservation-only event in January 2027. It is not the same as a daytime village walk, and it is not something you can add to a same-day Kanazawa sprint. Official pages say there is no ticket sales on the day, and that a national cold wave can cancel the event. This page is a planning note from those official sources, checked 22 November 2026 — recheck the association site before you pay.

Official dates (four evenings only)

The village office and the official tourism association both list these four sessions in Ogimachi, each from 17:30 to 19:30. 11 January is a Monday holiday because of a three-day weekend; the other three dates are Sundays.

The village says the event is held only four times, taking snow conditions and sunset into account. Admission tickets are valid only on the colour-coded date printed on them.

How official participation works

You must reserve in advance and obtain an admission ticket through one of five official methods. The tourism association itself sells only the first three. Tickets let you walk the lit settlement; they do not, by themselves, include the observatory.

  1. Stay in the village that night on an official lodging plan that includes an observatory ticket. The association ran this as a lottery (applications 1–31 October 2026; winners notified 10 November). Further places appear only if winners cancel — watch the official site, do not treat the closed lottery as still open.
  2. Reserve a standard-car space at the official light-up car park (Midashima). No observatory ticket. First-come, not a lottery. The first sale (10 September 2026) sold out. The second sale opens at 13:00 on 1 December 2026 on the association website, credit card only. Official prices for the second sale: 7,000 yen per car for 1–5 people, or 10,000 yen for 6–10 (maximum 10 people per car). Arrival from 15:00; leave by 20:00. Microbuses, camping cars and other special vehicles are not accepted.
  3. Arrive by taxi and reserve the designated car park (no observatory ticket). On event days, the association says Hakusan Taxi in the village is not operating because of the event.
  4. Join a tour sold by a scheduled-bus operator (Nohi, Kaetsuno, Toyama Chiho, Hokutetsu or Dolphin Traffic). Observatory tickets may or may not be included — read the operator’s own tour page.
  5. Join a tour sold by a travel company (no observatory ticket, according to the association).

From 15:30 a gate is in place. Without an event ticket you cannot enter the village. If you are only visiting in the daytime on an event date, the association says ordinary cars may use the daytime car park from 8:00, with last entry at 15:00 and a complete exit by 17:00; tour buses last enter at 14:00 and must leave by 16:00. Daytime buses should arrive by 15:20.

What to wear, and winter visits on other days

The association says light-up evenings are the coldest part of the night, with temperatures from about −10°C to 0°C and icy ground. It recommends warm, waterproof boots, spikes, and a flashlight, and requires studless winter tyres on cars. Drones are prohibited in this living village, including during the event.

A winter daytime visit on a non-event day still uses the ordinary Nohi / partner highway bus and the official manners page. Buses can be delayed by two hours or more in heavy snow. I do not recommend a same-day Kanazawa–Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa return in any season, and still less on a light-up evening.

Frequently asked questions

Can I just turn up on the night of the light-up?
No. The official Shirakawa-go Tourism Association page says the 2027 event is completely reservation-only, with an admission-ticket system, and that tickets are not sold on the day. Without a ticket you will be turned away.
Do regular Nohi bus tickets get me into the light-up?
Not by themselves. Event admission has to come through one of the five official participation methods. A regular highway-bus ticket is for transport only. On event days, the association says a gate is in place from 15:30, and daytime buses should arrive by 15:20 if you are not holding an event ticket.
How do I get a ticket that includes the observatory?
The association says observatory tickets are not sold on their own. They come with a village lodging plan booked through the official process, or with certain route-bus operator tours. A reserved car-park place does not include the observatory.
Is a winter visit still possible on other days?
Yes. The village is open for ordinary daytime visits outside the four event evenings, subject to official manners rules and winter road conditions. Nohi warns that buses can be delayed by two hours or more in heavy snow.
