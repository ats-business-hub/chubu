Jigokudani snow monkeys from Nagoya in winter: hours, fees and the rail route (2026–27)

Hours, fees and access times from the park’s official pages and Nagaden’s fare and ticket pages, checked 26 November 2026. Not a hands-on test.

Jigokudani Yaen-koen, in Yamanouchi, Nagano, is the park known for Japanese macaques bathing in a hot-spring pool in winter. The operator says the monkeys use the baths to stay warm in winter, and that they do not use them all day or all year. Appearances are not guaranteed. This is a researched route from official pages, not a trip report.

Related: Nagoya to Matsumoto in winter · Matsumoto Castle.

Winter opening hours

The official English Guide page, checked on 26 November 2026, lists winter hours (November–March) as 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., and summer hours (April–October) as 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. It asks visitors to arrive at least 30 minutes before closing, and to be ready to leave at closing time. The park says it has no set closing days, but may shorten hours or close without notice because of weather, the monkeys’ behaviour, or natural disasters. A park notice dated 31 October 2026 says winter operating hours begin on Sunday, 1 November — and 1 November 2026 was a Sunday.

The official route from Nagoya

The park’s English Access page groups Osaka and Nagoya together. From Shin-Osaka it shows the Tokaido Shinkansen Nozomi to Nagoya in 52 minutes, then the JR Chuo Line limited express from Nagoya to Nagano in 2 hours 43 minutes. If you start in Nagoya, the official segments onward are:

  • JR Chuo Line limited express (Nagoya to Nagano): 2 hours 43 minutes
  • Nagano Dentetsu limited express (Nagano to Yudanaka): 38 minutes
  • Bus: 15 minutes
  • Walking: 35 minutes

Adding those official Nagoya-onward segment times — 2 hours 43 minutes + 38 minutes + 15 minutes + 35 minutes — comes to 4 hours 11 minutes. That sum does not include waiting or transfer time, so a real door-to-door trip takes longer. The same page also lists a 41-minute express bus to Shiga Kogen as an alternative to the Nagaden-plus-local-bus legs. It lists 30 minutes on foot from Kanbayashi Onsen, and 15 minutes on foot from the Jigokudani parking lot. Use the time that matches the path you actually walk.

This article does not list Nagaden departure times. The full-line timetable PDF still carried a March 2021 date when the draft was prepared, so those times were left out. Check Nagaden’s current timetable or reservation site before you go.

Nagaden tickets

Nagaden’s fare table dated 1 December 2025 (the table found on the official site on 26 November 2026) lists an adult ordinary fare of 1,660 yen for the 33–34 km band. The station kilometre table on the same PDF puts Nagano to Yudanaka at 33.1 km, so the adult ordinary fare on that section is 1,660 yen under that table. No later fare revision was found on that official PDF. Limited-express surcharges, on the ticket page, are 100 yen for adults and 50 yen for children, regardless of distance. A reserved seat is 300 yen for adults and children alike. A private room on the 2100-series “Snow Monkey” set is 1,200 yen per room (up to four people), plus the base fare and limited-express ticket, and can be reserved from 31 days before departure until 10 minutes before the train leaves its starting station.

Nagaden’s SNOW MONKEY PASS (train, a local-bus return and one park admission) is sold only from 1 April 2026 to 30 September 2026. It is not a winter 2026–27 ticket.

The walk, park rules, and parking

The Access page says the path from Kanbayashi Onsen to Jigokudani is about 2 km and takes about 30 minutes, and that it is mostly flat with some hills. From the Jigokudani parking lot the walk is 15 minutes. The Guide page says there are no paved roads inside or outside the park, and that wheelchairs and carts cannot be used. Pets, including assistance dogs, are not allowed; the park notes that dogs are a natural enemy of the monkeys. Do not feed or touch the monkeys. The park is for watching them, not a human bath; bathing with the monkeys is prohibited. The Jigokudani parking lot costs 500 yen.

Combining the park with Matsumoto

Matsumoto sits on the Shinano route from Nagoya. If you split the trip, see Nagoya to Matsumoto by train in winter and Matsumoto Castle. A same-day return from Nagoya is a long day once you add transfers, the walk, and winter daylight; the 4 hours 11 minutes figure above is only the sum of official segment times, with no transfers included.

Frequently asked questions

What are the winter hours?
The official Guide page lists winter hours (November–March) as 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Arrive at least 30 minutes before closing. A 31 October 2026 notice said winter hours began on Sunday, 1 November 2026.
How much is admission?
As of 26 November 2026 the official Guide page lists 800 yen for adults (18+) and 400 yen for children aged 6–17. The same page says fees will be revised on 1 December 2026; this article does not transcribe the new amounts from the official notice images. Recheck before you go on or after that date.
How long does it take from Nagoya?
The park’s Access page lists 2 hours 43 minutes on the Chuo Line limited express from Nagoya to Nagano, 38 minutes on Nagaden to Yudanaka, 15 minutes by bus and 35 minutes on foot. Those official segments add up to 4 hours 11 minutes, not including transfers.
Is the SNOW MONKEY PASS valid in winter?
No. Nagaden’s ticket page lists the sales period as 1 April 2026 to 30 September 2026.
