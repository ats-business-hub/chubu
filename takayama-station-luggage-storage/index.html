Where to leave a suitcase at JR Takayama Station, from official and operator pages. Not a hands-on test.

Coin lockers at Takayama Station

The official Hida Takayama tourism site’s station page (last updated 3 July 2024) lists two locker areas. Both are available 24 hours a day, for up to 3 days including the first day, and take cash only.

LocationSML
i-CAFE111 lockers, 400 yen54 lockers, 600 yen20 lockers, 700 yen
Nohi Bus Center56 lockers, 400 yen32 lockers, 600 yen14 lockers, 700 yen

Because that page dates from 2024, counts, sizes, and prices may have changed. The tourism site’s other language versions do not list the same sizes, so I only give the S/M/L figures from the English page. Large suitcases may not fit a locker. The lockers are cash only, so bring coins.

Sagawa Express counter (staffed storage and delivery)

Sagawa’s own page for its Hida Takayama Tourist Information Center luggage counter lists:

  • Hours: 9:00–15:00
  • Address: 1-2 Honmachi, Takayama
  • Storage: 300 yen per item per day up to 80 cm (total of three sides), 500 yen up to 140 cm, 800 yen for 141 cm and over
  • It also handles luggage delivery, including shipping abroad through a separate service

The tourism site’s older page lists different hours and a flat fee, so I go by Sagawa’s own page. On 26 November 2026, Sagawa’s online reservation showed a system-maintenance notice. Check Sagawa’s page, or ask at the counter, before you rely on it. The 15:00 close is early: if you plan to pick up bags after a late afternoon in the old town, a locker or another option may suit better.

TABINO Satellite (near the station)

TABINO Hotel’s Satellite at 6-36-1 Hanasato-machi is a station-front lounge open 10:00–19:00. Its page says it offers paid luggage storage for sightseeing, and storage is free for TABINO Hotel guests. The page doesn’t publish the storage price.

Lunasol.LSS (luggage storage shop)

Lunasol.LSS lists its store at 5-15-3 Hanasato-cho, open 10:00–17:00, and says it is about a 2-minute walk from the station. Its published prices:

  • Large suitcase: 1,500 yen per day
  • Small suitcase or large backpack: 1,000 yen per day
  • Small backpack or bag: 500 yen per day
  • Short stay: 500 yen per hour (up to 2 hours), extensions 200 yen per 30 minutes
  • Storage up to 30 days; shipping available at extra cost

The site also shows a post about preparing a new store at the same address. Confirm the current location and hours on its site before you walk over.

Other things to know

  • The city-run Hida Takayama tourist information office is right outside the East Exit, open 8:30–17:00 (until 18:30 April–October), closed 30 December–2 January. Its page doesn’t say it stores luggage, but staff can point you to current options.
  • Yamato Transport (Ta-Q-Bin) is listed on the tourism page for shipping luggage. Check Yamato’s own pages for current hours and rates.
  • The station ticket office is open 6:00–20:50 (tourism page, July 2024).
  • If you are heading to Shirakawa-go by bus, check that bus operator’s luggage rules. See Takayama to Shirakawa-go bus.
  • Leaving bags in Nagoya instead? Nagoya to Takayama covers luggage storage near Nagoya Station.

Once your bags are stored

The old town and morning markets are close by. Plan the day with Takayama in 2 days, or add a soak at Gero Onsen on the Hida line.

Frequently asked questions

Are there coin lockers at Takayama Station?
Yes. The official Hida Takayama tourism page (updated 3 July 2024) lists lockers at i-CAFE and the Nohi Bus Center: S 400 yen, M 600 yen, L 700 yen, available 24 hours, up to 3 days including the first day, cash only. Because the page dates from 2024, check on arrival.
Is there staffed luggage storage near Takayama Station?
Sagawa Express lists a luggage counter at the Hida Takayama Tourist Information Center, 1-2 Honmachi, open 9:00–15:00, at 300 / 500 / 800 yen per item per day by size (up to 80 cm, up to 140 cm, 141 cm and over, total of three sides). On 26 November 2026 its online reservation showed a maintenance notice, so check Sagawa’s page before you go. TABINO Satellite and Lunasol.LSS near the station also offer paid storage.
Can I pick up my bags in the evening?
Check closing times. Sagawa’s counter closes at 15:00, Lunasol.LSS lists 10:00–17:00, and TABINO Satellite’s lounge 10:00–19:00. The station lockers are listed as available 24 hours.
