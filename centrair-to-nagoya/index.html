Chubu Centrair Airport to Nagoya: train, bus, or taxi

Meitetsu in about 28 minutes, 980 yen plus a 450-yen μticket on the μSKY. Official fares first.

Chubu Centrair International Airport (NGO) sits on an island off Tokoname, south of Nagoya. For most travellers the Meitetsu train is the default: it leaves from the airport station next to the terminal and reaches Meitetsu Nagoya in about half an hour. Airport buses no longer run to Nagoya Station during the day, and a taxi costs far more. Fares below were checked on official pages on 25 November 2026 and are adult one-way prices in Japanese yen.

By train: Meitetsu (the default)

Meitetsu’s official airport-access page gives the adult fare from Central Japan International Airport Station to Meitetsu Nagoya as 980 yen. Its standard journey times are 28 minutes on the μSKY limited express, 36 minutes on a Limited Express, and 49 minutes on a Semi-Express. Kanayama, a transfer point for JR and the subway, is 910 yen and 24 minutes by μSKY.

  • μSKY: every car is a first-class (特別車) car, so you need a μticket in addition to the fare. Meitetsu’s English μticket page lists it at 450 yen; if you settle on board instead, Meitetsu charges 500 yen. μtickets go on sale a month before travel and are not valid after your train leaves — no changes or refunds if you miss it.
  • Limited Express: on Meitetsu’s 2200-series Limited Express trains, cars 1 and 2 are first class (μticket needed); the other cars are ordinary and need only the 980-yen fare.
  • Semi-Express and slower trains: fare only.

Meitetsu Nagoya connects to JR Nagoya Station (Shinkansen and JR lines) and the subway, according to Meitetsu’s line diagram. If you are heading to Takayama or Kanazawa, that is where you change. Meitetsu’s site also lists manaca IC cards and “Tap to Ride” (クレカ乗車) options; check that your card works before you rely on it.

By bus: no daytime bus to Nagoya Station

On Centrair’s official bus page, the Centrair Limousine route to Nagoya Station (Meitetsu Bus Center) is marked “All bus services are suspended.” The limousine still runs to Sakae / Fushimi: about 55 minutes, 2,000 yen (Meitetsu Bus). Centrair also lists Fujigaoka (1,800 yen) and Aichi Commemorative Park for Ghibli Park (about 85 minutes, 2,000 yen).

For very late arrivals or early flights, Nagoya Bus runs an Airport Liner to Nagoya Station West Exit. Its official page lists airport departures at 0:30 and 7:00 only, an adult fare of 1,500 yen, and cash only. Buses leave from Terminal 1 Access Plaza, 1st floor: stop 6 for Sakae–Fushimi and stop 7 for Nagoya Station West Gate.

By taxi

Centrair’s taxi page estimates Nagoya Station at about 50 minutes and 16,000 yen, and Sakae at about 45 minutes and 15,500 yen. It says expressway charges, including the Centrair Bridge, are paid by the passenger, and that the table is a guide only. The taxi rank is on the 1st floor of Access Plaza for Terminal 1.

Pre-booked private transfer

If you land late, travel as a family or group with luggage, or want a driver waiting in the arrivals hall, a pre-booked transfer gives you a price before you fly. Compare that quote with the airport’s taxi estimate (about 16,000 yen plus tolls to Nagoya Station) and with the 1,430-yen μSKY. I am not quoting transfer prices; they depend on vehicle, time, and destination.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get from Chubu Centrair Airport to Nagoya Station?
Take the Meitetsu train from the airport station to Meitetsu Nagoya, next to JR Nagoya Station. As of 25 November 2026 the adult fare is 980 yen; the μSKY limited express takes about 28 minutes and needs a 450-yen μticket (1,430 yen total).
Is there a bus from Centrair to Nagoya Station?
Not during the day. Centrair’s official bus page marks the Nagoya Station (Meitetsu Bus Center) limousine as suspended. The limousine runs to Sakae and Fushimi (about 55 minutes, 2,000 yen), and Nagoya Bus runs two Airport Liner departures to Nagoya Station West Exit at 0:30 and 7:00 (1,500 yen, cash).
How much is a taxi from Centrair to Nagoya?
Centrair estimates about 50 minutes and 16,000 yen to Nagoya Station, plus expressway tolls including the Centrair Bridge. It is a guide, not a fixed fare.
Do I need a μticket for every Meitetsu airport train?
Only for first-class cars. Every car on the μSKY is first class, so you need a μticket. On a Limited Express only cars 1 and 2 are first class; the other cars need just the 980-yen fare.
