Takayama to Kanazawa by highway bus

Official Nohi fares and the 1 December 2026 timetable change, checked 25 November 2026. Confirm whether your bus stops at Shirakawa-go. Updated 25 November 2026: booking note for the direct bus clarified.

Official operator pages remain the primary place to book. I do not recommend day-tripping Shirakawa-go from Kanazawa and back, and Japan Bus Tickets does not sell this village line.

The usual way from Takayama to Kanazawa is the reserved highway bus on the Nohi / Hokutetsu Shirakawa-go line. You can stop in the village, or, from 1 December 2026, take a new direct bus that skips Shirakawa-go. Fares and the timetable change on that date. Figures below are from Nohi’s official English page and its official fare- and timetable-revision notices, rechecked on 25 November 2026.

The short version

  • Want to see Shirakawa-go on the way? Book the Shirakawa-go line, not the new direct bus. Confirm the stop on your ticket.
  • Just going city to city from 1 December 2026? Nohi adds a direct Takayama–Kanazawa bus that does not stop at the village: 9:10 from Takayama Bus Terminal, arriving Kanazawa Station at 11:10, and 15:00 from Kanazawa Station, arriving Takayama at 17:00. The official notice describes reservation-only operation for the restored Takayama→Shirakawa-go trip; it does not spell out the booking rule for this direct bus, so book ahead or check Nohi’s official page for your date.
  • Adult one-way fares on Nohi’s official tables: Takayama–Kanazawa 4,200 yen through 30 November 2026 and 4,400 yen from 1 December 2026. Takayama–Shirakawa-go 2,800 yen, then 2,900 yen. Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa 2,800 yen, then 2,900 yen.
  • The Takayama–Hokuriku Area Pass covers the listed buses on this line (some exceptions; reservations still required). The nationwide Japan Rail Pass does not.
  • Japan Bus Tickets does not sell this village / Kanazawa line. Use Japan Bus Online or the official Nohi booking path.

Child rules are on that official page: a child under 6 rides free unless they occupy a seat. Recheck before you travel. Nohi also warns that delays of two hours or more can occur because of village parking queues, heavy snow, or expressway closures.

The 1 December 2026 direct bus

Nohi’s official timetable-revision notice (dated for service from Tuesday, 1 December 2026) adds a Takayama Bus Center–Kanazawa direct bus that does not stop at Shirakawa-go. On the official English timetable marked “Effective Dec 1, 2026,” that outbound trip leaves Takayama Bus Terminal at 9:10 and arrives at Kanazawa Station at 11:10. The return leaves Kanazawa Station at 15:00 and arrives at Takayama Bus Terminal at 17:00. If the village is your destination, do not book this departure.

Buses that do stop at Shirakawa-go continue on the same official timetable. Times change by departure, and some columns are reservation-only. Always match the official timetable for your date rather than memorising a single time.

How long does it take?

On the official timetable valid through 30 November 2026, a typical reserved departure is the 7:50 from Takayama Bus Terminal, arriving at Kanazawa Station at 10:05, with a stop at Shirakawa-go (Ogimachi). Later reserved trips run through the afternoon; the official table lists a last Kanazawa arrival at 18:45. From 1 December, the new direct bus is about two hours. Recheck the official table for your date, including weekend and holiday notes.

Is there a train instead?

There is no direct JR train from Takayama to Kanazawa. The rail option is usually the Limited Express Hida to Toyama, then the Hokuriku Shinkansen to Kanazawa. The Area Pass maps both of those rail sections, with the reserved-seat rules already described on the pass comparison. The bus is the straightforward same-corridor choice, and it is the one that can stop at Shirakawa-go. I have not invented a point-to-point train total here; compare official JR fares for your date if you prefer that route.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the bus from Takayama to Kanazawa?
Nohi’s official English fare table lists the adult one-way fare as 4,200 yen through 30 November 2026 and 4,400 yen from 1 December 2026. Recheck the official table before you travel.
Does the Takayama–Kanazawa bus stop at Shirakawa-go?
Most departures on the official Shirakawa-go line do. From 1 December 2026, Nohi also runs a direct Takayama–Kanazawa bus that does not stop at the village (9:10 from Takayama, 15:00 from Kanazawa on the official notice and English timetable). Confirm the stop on your booking.
Does the Takayama–Hokuriku Area Pass cover this bus?
Yes, on the listed Nohi/Hokutetsu Shirakawa-go and related lines, with some exceptions. Official pass conditions still require a reservation. The nationwide Japan Rail Pass does not cover these buses.
Can I book this bus on Japan Bus Tickets?
No. Japan Bus Tickets does not sell the Nohi Takayama–Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa village line. Use Japan Bus Online or the reservation path on Nohi’s official page.
Is there a direct train?
No. A rail trip is usually the Limited Express Hida to Toyama and the Hokuriku Shinkansen to Kanazawa. Compare official JR fares and times for your date. The bus is the direct corridor, and it is the option that can stop at Shirakawa-go.
