Nabana no Sato illumination from Nagoya (2026–27): dates, tickets, hours and access

Nabana no Sato is a large flower park in Nagashima, Kuwana City, just over the Aichi–Mie border, run by the Nagashima Resort group. Its winter illumination is one of the easiest big evening outings from Nagoya. This guide covers only the illumination visit and is built from official pages. It is not a trip report. For other options, see Day trips from Nagoya.

2026–27 illumination period and closed days

The official illumination page and fee page list the 2026–27 season as 17 October 2026 to 31 May 2027, closed on 6–7 January 2027. The fee page also says the park stays open on rainy days, and that details may change without notice. Before you go, check the "latest operating status" page linked from the fee page.

Opening hours and when the lights come on

Nabana no Sato publishes opening hours day by day on its business calendar, not as one fixed season-wide time. The fee page says admission ends 30 minutes before closing.

The official pages checked for this article give no clock time for the light-up. The fee page has a "lighting time" heading, but no time appeared in the page text we checked. The illumination page's only lighting note is about the Twin Tree, the park's symbol tree, which lights up at the sound of the chapel bell. Check the calendar for your date, and don't rely on a light-up time from unofficial sources.

What you'll see

The illumination page groups the displays into named areas: a large theme area; a water illumination it calls one of the largest in Japan and the symbol of the event; a 200 m tunnel of light; a champagne-gold "world of light" with seasonal flower light-ups; flower beds lit in colour; and the Twin Tree.

Tickets in every period below include admission to the Begonia Garden greenhouse. The fee page mentions a special sound-and-light show in the Begonia Garden after the lights come on.

Children below elementary-school age are free in every period. The vouchers can be used for shopping and meals inside the park, though not in some ticket machines, and they have an expiry date that depends on the ticket type and sales period. Credit cards, QR-code payments and e-money are accepted. Parking is free.

Christmas special days (23–25 December)

23–25 December run on different rules. You need a dated special ticket, and the number of tickets is limited. The park opens from 15:00, until 22:00 on 23 December and until 23:00 on 24 and 25 December. The illumination page says these tickets are sold at FamilyMart and 7-Eleven, and on the Asoview! booking site. Same-day window tickets are sold only if any remain, regular and advance tickets can't be used on these days, and the disability discount doesn't apply.

Buying tickets in advance

Advance tickets are sold through FamilyMart (JTB Leisure Ticket) and 7-Eleven (Seven Ticket) multi-copy machines nationwide. They are also sold at the shops in the Wangan Nagashima Parking Area on the Isewangan Expressway (both directions, 7:00–22:00, cash only, no dated Christmas tickets).

Official conditions to note:

  • The advance ticket lets you go straight to the gate without exchanging it, and you can buy it on the day you use it.
  • You can't change the ticket type or get a refund.
  • Without a ticket, issuing one at the park's counter can take 30 minutes or more.
  • The park takes no responsibility for resold tickets or tickets from unofficial channels.

From Nagoya by direct bus (Meitetsu Bus Center)

The direct bus is the simplest option with no transfers. Nabana no Sato's access page gives about 30 minutes from Meitetsu Bus Center. You board at 4F, bay 22, and tickets are sold on 3F. The route is run jointly by Meitetsu Bus and Mie Kotsu.

Fares. Nabana no Sato's own pages disagree on this fare (one page says ¥1,000 adult, the fare table says ¥1,200). The operators settle it. Meitetsu Bus's route page lists ¥1,200 adult and ¥600 child one way until 13 December 2026. Its 6 November 2026 notice says the fare rises to ¥1,300 adult and ¥650 child from Monday, 14 December 2026, and the round-trip ticket (valid 2 days) goes from ¥2,400 to ¥2,600 for adults. Mie Kotsu's fare search also showed ¥1,200 on 26 November 2026. A one-way ticket bought on or before 13 December is valid only on the day of purchase.

Things to know before you queue:

  • No standing. Standing passengers aren't allowed, so when a bus is full you wait for the next one.
  • Not every departure stops at Nabana. The 8:00 departure from Meitetsu Bus Center and the 9:30 departure from Nagashima Onsen skip Nabana no Sato, and so do extra direct buses to Nagashima Onsen. Check that your bus is marked for Nabana no Sato.
  • IC cards. Nationwide transit IC cards such as manaca, TOICA, Suica and ICOCA work on this route. Mie Kotsu's emica card works only on buses operated by Mie Kotsu. IC fares are charged per ride, so round-trip discounts don't apply.

Departure times change by day type. Use the timetable linked from the illumination access page or Mie Kotsu's timetable search.

From Nagoya by Kintetsu train + bus

Nabana no Sato's train page calls this the fastest train-and-bus route.

Step 1: Kintetsu Nagoya to Kintetsu Nagashima. The train page gives about 17 minutes by express (when the express makes its temporary stop at Kintetsu Nagashima) or about 25 minutes by semi-express. On weekdays it says to take a semi-express or local train, since all of them stop at Kintetsu Nagashima. If you board an express that doesn't stop there, change to a local at Kintetsu Yatomi. Some express trains make temporary stops at Kintetsu Nagashima on weekends and holidays during the illumination. Kintetsu, Mie Kotsu and Nagashima Kanko Kaihatsu announced this in a joint release on 11 September 2026.

Step 2: Bus from Kintetsu Nagashima. Mie Kotsu runs a direct bus, the Nagashima Nabana no Sato Line, from Kintetsu Nagashima Ekimae to Nabana no Sato every day of the illumination period (except the closed days, 6–7 January 2027). It takes about 10 minutes, with one to three buses an hour. Mie Kotsu's 1 October 2026 notice lists the fare as ¥250 adult and ¥130 child, and the joint release says nationwide IC cards and emica are accepted. One Nabana page still shows ¥230 / ¥120; the operator's figure is the one to use. From 30 December 2026 to 4 January 2027, the bus runs on the weekend and holiday timetable.

Don't plan on a bus from Kuwana Station. Mie Kotsu says the Kuwana Station–Nabana no Sato route bus is suspended every day during the illumination period. Take the train to Kintetsu Nagashima instead.

Discount set ticket. The fee page mentions a Kintetsu train-and-bus round-trip set ticket. The train page says it is sold at the ticket windows of major Kintetsu stations. A separate train + bus + illumination admission deal ticket is also listed there. We didn't confirm either price, so ask at the window.

Arriving by Shinkansen

If you arrive at Nagoya by Shinkansen, switch to Kintetsu at Nagoya for the route above, or walk to Meitetsu Bus Center for the direct bus. If you're weighing passes, see Japan Rail Pass vs Central Japan regional passes.

Driving and parking

Parking is free, with space for 5,700 cars. The access page says it's about 30 minutes from Nagoya by expressway (Wangan Nagashima IC or Nagashima IC). It recommends Route 23 or the Isewangan Expressway for arrival and warns that Route 1 and the Higashi-Meihan Expressway are expected to be congested. The park also asks visitors to use public transport where possible.

From Centrair Airport

The direct bus between Centrair and Nabana no Sato is suspended for the time being. If you land at Chubu Centrair, get into Nagoya first (Chubu Centrair Airport to Nagoya), then use the direct bus or the Kintetsu route.

Planning tips

  • Buy your ticket before you leave Nagoya. It avoids the possible 30-minute-plus wait at the gate, and it's cheaper than the window price for most of the season.
  • Check the calendar for your date. Closing time varies by day, and entry stops 30 minutes before closing.
  • If you ride the direct bus after 13 December, budget ¥1,300 each way.
  • Weekend evenings get busy. With no standing on the direct bus, you may wait for the next departure. The Kintetsu route is a useful backup.
  • Stay connected. Bus queues and timetables are easier with mobile data. See Japan eSIM for Central Japan.

Frequently asked questions

When is the 2026–27 Nabana no Sato illumination?
The official illumination page lists 17 October 2026 to 31 May 2027, closed on 6 and 7 January 2027.
How much is the ticket?
From 21 November to 22 December and from 26 December to 28 February, the official fee page lists ¥3,000 at convenience stores and ¥3,200 at the gate, each with a ¥1,000 park voucher and Begonia Garden entry. 23–25 December needs a dated ¥5,500 ticket with a ¥2,000 voucher. Children below elementary-school age are free.
What time do the lights come on?
The official pages checked on 26 November 2026 do not give a clock time for the light-up. Opening hours are published day by day on the business calendar, and entry ends 30 minutes before closing.
How much is the direct bus from Nagoya?
Meitetsu Bus lists ¥1,200 adult and ¥600 child one way from Meitetsu Bus Center until 13 December 2026, and ¥1,300 adult and ¥650 child from 14 December 2026. The ride takes about 30 minutes and standing is not allowed.
Is there a bus from Kuwana Station?
No. Mie Kotsu says the Kuwana Station–Nabana no Sato bus is suspended during the illumination period. Take Kintetsu to Kintetsu Nagashima and the direct bus from there (about 10 minutes, ¥250 adult).
