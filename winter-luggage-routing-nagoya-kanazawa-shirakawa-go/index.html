It does not plan your sightseeing. For the trips themselves, see Shirakawa-go day trip from Nagoya, Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go, Nagoya to Takayama, Nagoya to Kanazawa and Takayama to Shirakawa-go bus.

The short version: four ways to handle a suitcase on this route

On this corridor the suitcase usually decides the plan. Almost every leg into Shirakawa-go is a bus, and buses share a limited luggage trunk. Most travellers end up with one of these patterns:

  • Route A: carry everything one way. Nagoya → Takayama → Shirakawa-go → Kanazawa (or the reverse). Your bags ride on two or three vehicles, so trunk rules matter most.
  • Route B: Takayama as a base. Leave the big case in Takayama and day-trip to Shirakawa-go with a small bag.
  • Route C: Kanazawa as a base. The same idea from the Hokuriku side.
  • Route D: store the case in Nagoya or Kanazawa. Do the mountain section with carry-on only and collect the case on your way back.

Which pattern suits you depends on how many bus legs your suitcase would ride, and on whether your dates fall in the holiday gap or on a Shirakawa-go Light-Up evening (see the winter section below).

What the official bus baggage rules say

Free carry-on limits on Nohi Bus. Nohi Bus's English FAQ sets the free limits for luggage brought on board at up to 10 kg, within 0.027 cubic metres in volume and within 1 metre in length (nouhibus.co.jp). For scale, 0.027 m³ is a 30 × 30 × 30 cm cube (editor's arithmetic, not an official figure). A full-size check-in suitcase is therefore a trunk item, not a cabin item.

Trunk space. The same FAQ says highway buses and some sightseeing route buses have a trunk under the floor. Nohi Bus sets no fixed limit on how many items you put in the trunk or how big they are, but it asks passengers to keep luggage to a minimum. You load and unload your own bags, and staff may help if needed. These rules apply only to Nohi Bus; for other operators, contact them directly (nouhibus.co.jp).

Large items may be refused. The route pages are stricter in tone. The Shirakawa-go route page says the trunk is shared by many passengers and that large items may not fit (nouhibus.co.jp; Japanese version nouhibus.co.jp). The Nagoya–Takayama page says the same thing in Japanese (nouhibus.co.jp). The English highway-bus page adds that trunk items may be refused depending on size, and tells you to check with the operating company before boarding (nouhibus.co.jp). No route page prints a size or weight limit for suitcases.

Your bus may not be a Nohi Bus. Five companies run the Takayama–Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa/Toyama/Takaoka services: Nohi Bus, Hokuriku Tetsudo, Kaetsunou Bus, Iruka Kotsu and Toyama Chihou Tetsudou (nouhibus.co.jp). Nagoya–Takayama is run jointly by Nohi Bus, Meitetsu Bus and JR Tokai Bus (nouhibus.co.jp). The Nohi Bus FAQ limits above are confirmed only for Nohi Bus. For other operators' buses, contact the operator before you travel.

Items that must not go in the trunk: - Keep mobile batteries in the cabin and in sight (nouhibus.co.jp; nouhibus.co.jp). - Do not put valuables, fragile items or perishables in the trunk (nouhibus.co.jp). - Bear spray is not allowed at your seat. It has to be sealed in a plastic bag and placed in the trunk (nouhibus.co.jp; nouhibus.co.jp). - Do not put heavy or unstable items on the overhead racks (nouhibus.co.jp). - Operators do not accept liability for damage, loss or theft of baggage (nouhibus.co.jp; nouhibus.co.jp).

Rail legs: where Shinkansen baggage rules apply and where they don't

JR Central's oversized-baggage rule covers the Tokaido, Sanyo, Kyushu and Nishi-Kyushu Shinkansen. It does not apply to conventional lines, where no baggage reservation is needed (global.jr-central.co.jp). On the Tokaido Shinkansen: - Bags up to 160 cm (length + width + height) need no reservation. - Bags of 161–250 cm need a reserved seat that comes with an oversized baggage area. - Bags over 250 cm are not allowed. - Carrying an oversized bag without that reservation costs 1,000 yen including tax (global.jr-central.co.jp).

This matters only if your trip starts with a Shinkansen ride into Nagoya. The Limited Express Hida runs on the Takayama Line, a conventional line, so the Shinkansen baggage reservation rule does not apply to it. The Hokuriku Shinkansen into Kanazawa is not on JR Central's list. Check its baggage rules with the operator, JR West, before you travel.

For winter, JR Central's 22 October 2026 release says that from 25 December to 5 January the Hida runs with all seats reserved and no unreserved seats. It also lists 100 extra Hida trains between 1 December 2026 and 28 February 2027, mostly over year-end/New Year and on January and February weekends (jr-central.co.jp). The release says nothing about where luggage goes on the Hida. Ask JR Central if you need details on luggage space aboard.

Route A: Nagoya → Takayama → Shirakawa-go → Kanazawa with everything

This is the classic one-way crossing, and your suitcase does the most work on it.

Nagoya → Takayama by highway bus. Buses leave from the Meitetsu Bus Center and JR Nagoya Station and arrive at Takayama Nohi Bus Center. The trip takes about 2 hours 45 minutes. The one-way fare is 3,600 yen, or 3,300 yen with the web discount (nouhibus.co.jp). Booking rules: - All seats are reserved, and booking opens one month before the travel date. - Online bookings must be paid within 3 days, counting the booking day, or they are cancelled. - Web-fare purchases close at 24:00 the day before departure. - Round-trip tickets are not sold on board. - If the Nagoya route is full, the page points to the Gifu–Takayama route instead.

(All from nouhibus.co.jp.) The fares were checked on 27 November 2026; check the page again before booking.

Nagoya → Takayama by rail. The Hida keeps your bag inside the train rather than in a shared trunk. Over the 25 December–5 January period it is all-reserved (jr-central.co.jp). Fares and journey times belong on Nagoya to Takayama.

Takayama → Shirakawa-go → Kanazawa. From 1 December 2026 the one-way fares are (nouhibus.co.jp): - Takayama Bus Terminal → Shirakawa-go: 2,900 yen - Shirakawa-go → Kanazawa Station: 2,900 yen - Takayama Bus Terminal → Kanazawa Station: 4,400 yen

The Kanazawa stop is Kanazawa Station West Exit (金沢駅西口) (nouhibus.co.jp). The English page lists about 50 minutes from Takayama Nohi Bus Center to Shirakawa-go, with 16 round trips a day (nouhibus.co.jp). Timetables and fares are revised from 1 December (nouhibus.co.jp), so check the winter timetable for the number of trips on your date.

Some services on this route need a reservation (nouhibus.co.jp): - Reservations open one month ahead (nouhibus.co.jp). - You can book online through Japan Bus Online between 5:00 and 2:00. - Mobile tickets work only on non-reserved buses. - Bus-pass holders can book reserved-seat buses only by phone: 0577-32-1688, 9:00–17:00 (nouhibus.co.jp).

Luggage logic for Route A. If you are carrying a full-size case into the village, you have to store it there for the time between buses. No official Shirakawa-go tourism page reviewed for this article gives luggage storage or coin-locker details, so this guide can't confirm availability, prices or hours there. Ask the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association: +81-5769-6-1013, 9:00–17:00 (shirakawa-go.gr.jp). Another option is to store the big case (Route D) and cross the mountains with a small bag.

Near Nagoya Station you can compare luggage storage spots on Radical Storage (affiliate link, #ad).

Route B: Base in Takayama, day trip to Shirakawa-go

If you sleep in Takayama, the simplest plan is to leave the suitcase in Takayama and ride to Shirakawa-go with a daypack. Then the trunk only matters on the way in from Nagoya. Leave it at your hotel, or use storage at Takayama Station. Locker sizes, staffed storage and how they work in winter are covered on Takayama Station luggage storage. For winter conditions in town, see Takayama in winter.

Takayama Nohi Bus Center is where both the Nagoya highway bus and the Shirakawa-go route buses stop (nouhibus.co.jp; nouhibus.co.jp), so you rarely have to drag a case far between connections. Continuing to Kanazawa afterwards? See Takayama to Kanazawa by highway bus.

Route C: Base in Kanazawa, round trip to Shirakawa-go

From the Hokuriku side the logic is the same. Leave the case at your Kanazawa hotel and do Shirakawa-go as a round trip from Kanazawa Station West Exit. The fare is 2,900 yen each way from 1 December 2026 (nouhibus.co.jp). The Kanazawa City Tourism Association's access page covers arrival into Kanazawa, not station facilities (kanazawa-kankoukyoukai.or.jp), so this guide does not cover coin lockers or storage at Kanazawa Station. Ask at the station or your hotel.

Booking contacts for the Kanazawa side: - Hokuriku Tetsudo: 076-234-0123, 9:00–18:00 - Nohi Bus: 0577-32-1688, 9:00–17:00

(Both from nouhibus.co.jp.) The route walkthrough is on Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go.

Route D: Leave the big case behind

If you're doing Nagoya → mountains → back to Nagoya, or flying in and out of Centrair, consider leaving the large suitcase in Nagoya and travelling with carry-on only. Your bag then stays within the Nohi Bus free carry-on limits of up to 10 kg, within 0.027 m³ and within 1 m (nouhibus.co.jp), and you avoid the "large items may be refused" risk on trunk-dependent buses.

Storage marketplaces list shops and hotels that hold bags for a fee. This guide does not list their fees; check the price for each location when you book.

Storage marketplaces such as Radical Storage list locations around Nagoya and other cities (affiliate link, #ad).

Winter risks for luggage: delays, holiday gaps and Light-Up dates

Delays. Nohi Bus warns that buses on the Shirakawa-go route can be delayed by two hours or more, even after detouring. The causes it lists are queues of private cars waiting to park at Shirakawa-go, heavy snow and expressway closures (nouhibus.co.jp; nouhibus.co.jp). If you are collecting a stored bag on the other side, check the store's closing time and leave a buffer.

Holiday gaps. Some services do not run on weekends, holidays, 13–15 August or 29 December–3 January. On weekends, holidays and those dates, some buses also skip the Iijima and Hatogaya stops (nouhibus.co.jp). The Gokayama Suganuma stop is served only from 1 April to 30 November (nouhibus.co.jp), so it is not available in winter.

Light-Up dates. The 2027 Shirakawa-go Light-Up is on 11, 17, 24 and 31 January. The additional Light-Up accommodation reservations are sold out (shirakawa-go.gr.jp). On those evenings, plan for full buses and busy trunks, and travel light if you can.

Check service status before you go. The Nohi Bus FAQ points to its "Bus Service Information" page for current service status (nouhibus.co.jp). Having mobile data on the road makes checking easier; see Japan eSIM for Central Japan, Pocket Wi-Fi, SIM, or eSIM at Chubu Centrair Airport and Japan eSIM vs pocket Wi-Fi for Chubu.

If you need a SIM, eSIM or pocket Wi-Fi, Sakura Mobile, Japan Wireless and NINJA WiFi are options (affiliate links, #ad). I have not tested these services.

Pre-departure checklist

  • Count your bus legs. Each one is a point where a large item could be refused.
  • Weigh and measure your cabin bag against 10 kg, 0.027 m³ and 1 m (nouhibus.co.jp).
  • Mobile batteries go in your cabin bag; valuables and fragile items stay out of the trunk.
  • Reservations open one month ahead; book reserved seats early where you can (nouhibus.co.jp; nouhibus.co.jp).
  • Check whether your dates fall in 29 December–3 January or on a Light-Up date.
  • Riding the Tokaido Shinkansen with a case over 160 cm? Reserve a seat with an oversized baggage area (global.jr-central.co.jp).
  • Confirm storage at both ends, including closing times.

If you'd rather leave the transfers to someone else, a guided tour is an option.

Frequently asked questions

Can I bring a full-size suitcase on the bus from Takayama to Shirakawa-go?
It usually goes in the shared trunk, not the cabin. Route pages warn that large items may be refused because trunk space is limited (nouhibus.co.jp). No size limit for the trunk is published.
What are the Nohi Bus carry-on limits?
Up to 10 kg, within 0.027 m³ and within 1 m in length (nouhibus.co.jp). These apply to Nohi Bus; for other operators on the route, contact the operator.
Are there coin lockers at Shirakawa-go?
No official page we checked lists them. Contact the Shirakawa-go Tourist Association on +81-5769-6-1013, 9:00–17:00 (shirakawa-go.gr.jp).
Do I need a baggage reservation on the Hida train?
JR Central's oversized-baggage reservation applies to certain Shinkansen lines, not to conventional lines (global.jr-central.co.jp). The Hida is all-reserved from 25 December to 5 January (jr-central.co.jp).
Do Shirakawa-go buses run over New Year?
Some services do not run from 29 December to 3 January (nouhibus.co.jp). Check the timetable for your exact bus.
