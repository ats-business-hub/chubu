Why Inuyama Castle makes a good day trip

Inuyama Castle is a short Meitetsu ride north of Nagoya and makes an easy half-day or full-day trip. The official English site is titled "Inuyama Castle, National Treasure" (https://inuyamajo.jp/). Japan's national tourism organization notes that the keep is a designated National Treasure and that it overlooks the Kiso River from a hilltop (https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1246/). JNTO's page also says the castle was built in 1537 and calls its tower the oldest surviving castle tower in Japan (https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1246/). The official castle sites checked for this guide don't print a construction year.

Unlike Nagoya Castle, whose keep can't currently be entered (see Nagoya Castle visitor guide), the keep at Inuyama is the main reason to go. The castle is popular: on 19 November 2026 the official Inuyama tourism site reported that the castle's annual visitors had passed 700,000 for the first time (https://inuyama.gr.jp/). This page covers only the castle and the official route from Nagoya. For other trips from the city, see Day trips from Nagoya.

Opening hours, last entry and closed days

  • Hours: 9:00 to 17:00 (https://inuyama-castle.jp/guidance/ ; https://inuyamajo.jp/visit/openinghours-and-entrancefee/index.html)
  • Last entry: 16:30 (https://inuyama-castle.jp/guidance/ ; https://inuyamajo.jp/visit/openinghours-and-entrancefee/index.html)
  • Closed: 29–31 December (https://inuyama-castle.jp/guidance/ ; https://inuyamajo.jp/visit/openinghours-and-entrancefee/index.html)

The official Japanese guidance page says entry may be limited when it's crowded (https://inuyama-castle.jp/guidance/). With visitor numbers this high, arriving early in the day is the simplest way to avoid waiting. No temporary closures were listed on the official English homepage when this guide was checked (https://inuyamajo.jp/).

Admission fees and set tickets

Individual and group fees from the official Japanese guidance page (https://inuyama-castle.jp/guidance/):

Category Individual Group 30+ Group 100+ Group 300+
General (adults) 1,000 yen 900 yen 800 yen 700 yen
Elementary and junior high school students 200 yen 180 yen 160 yen 140 yen
Preschool children Free — — —

The English site gives adults 1,000 yen and children 200 yen (https://inuyamajo.jp/visit/openinghours-and-entrancefee/index.html). It also says wheelchair users and one caretaker (two people in total) can enter free (https://inuyamajo.jp/visit/openinghours-and-entrancefee/index.html). Group fees are cash only (https://inuyama-castle.jp/guidance/). Tickets can't be reissued, changed or refunded once bought (https://inuyama-castle.jp/guidance/).

Payment: The ticket booth accepts transit IC cards, a range of e-money brands and QR payments (https://inuyama-castle.jp/guidance/). The English page lists transit IC cards, Rakuten Edy, QUICPay, PayPay, nanaco and WAON at the Front Gate Ticket Office (https://inuyamajo.jp/visit/openinghours-and-entrancefee/index.html).

Set tickets sold at the window, cash only (https://inuyama-castle.jp/guidance/):

  • Castle plus castle-town set: 1,200 yen
  • Castle plus Urakuen garden: 2,000 yen (general)
  • Castle plus Meiji Mura: 3,300 yen general, 850 yen elementary/junior high
  • Castle plus Little World: 3,000 yen general, 1,200 yen elementary/junior high

Getting there from Nagoya by Meitetsu

The official castle sites both describe the same basic route: take the Meitetsu Inuyama Line from Meitetsu Nagoya Station (NH36) to Inuyama Station (IY15). The trip takes around 25 minutes (https://inuyamajo.jp/visit/access/ ; https://inuyama-castle.jp/access/). The official Inuyama tourism site also gives about 25 minutes from Nagoya Station to Inuyama Station on Meitetsu (https://inuyama.gr.jp/).

Practical details from official pages:

  • Coming from JR Nagoya Station? The official English castle site gives about a 10-minute walk to Meitetsu Nagoya Station (https://inuyamajo.jp/visit/access/).
  • Train types: The English site names Rapid Limited Express or Limited Express for the roughly 25-minute trip (https://inuyamajo.jp/visit/access/). The Japanese access page also lists routes from stations such as Kanayama and Kami-Otai (https://inuyama-castle.jp/access/). Check the current train on Meitetsu's official timetable (https://www.meitetsu.co.jp/train/timetable/).
  • Platforms at Meitetsu Nagoya: Meitetsu's station page lists Platform 1 for trains toward Meitetsu Gifu, Tsushima and Inuyama, and Platform 2 for special (reserved) car boarding in the same direction (https://www.meitetsu.co.jp/train/station_info/line12/station/1201.html).
  • First-class car (μticket): Meitetsu's English page says that on cars needing one, a 450-yen μticket is required on top of the regular ticket, and it costs the same for all ages (https://www.meitetsu.co.jp/eng/).
  • Station help: Meitetsu Nagoya has a service center. It's open 10:00–19:00 on weekdays and 9:00–18:00 on weekends and holidays (https://www.meitetsu.co.jp/train/station_info/line12/station/1201.html).

Regular fare, Meitetsu Nagoya to Inuyama: The Meitetsu fare page and the official castle pages checked for this guide do not print a single figure. Meitetsu sends readers to its timetable and fare search (fare page; English site). Use that search for the current fare.

If you're deciding whether a rail pass is worth it for this trip, see Japan Rail Pass vs Central Japan regional passes.

The Inuyama Josai-machi Kippu (castle-town ticket)

Meitetsu sells a discount ticket for this trip, the Inuyama Josai-machi Kippu, and its official page prints a price. Details from Meitetsu's official campaign page (https://www.meitetsu.co.jp/plan/campaign/detail/1287701_8034.html):

  • What's included: a discounted Meitetsu round-trip ticket to Inuyama, an Inuyama Castle admission ticket, a discount ticket for Urakuen garden, and Inuyama castle-town coupons
  • Price from Meitetsu Nagoya: 1,950 yen (adult; tax included). Other stations have different prices, for example 2,050 yen from Kanayama.
  • No child version: the page says there's no price for junior high school students and younger
  • Validity: rail ticket, castle ticket and Urakuen discount are valid for 2 consecutive days from the start date. The castle-town coupons are valid until 1 April 2027.
  • Sales period: 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027. You can buy it on the day of travel.
  • Excluded dates: 29–31 December 2026 and 26 January 2027
  • Where to buy: sales points marked with the "沿線まち歩き" icon
  • Ride range: from the Meitetsu Nagoya side, the ticket covers travel as far as Inuyama-Yuen Station
  • Using it at the castle: hand the attached castle ticket to staff at the entrance
  • Restrictions: the Urakuen discount can't be used on the garden's closed days, and the coupons can be used at up to three shops but not at the castle or Urakuen

The ticket includes castle admission (1,000 yen for adults if bought on its own). Because the regular rail fare is not printed on the pages checked here, this guide does not say whether the kippu is cheaper than paying the train fare and castle ticket separately. Compare it with Meitetsu’s fare search on the day you travel.

From Inuyama Station to the castle

Both official castle sites give about 20 minutes on foot from Inuyama Station to the castle (English access; Japanese access). JNTO also mentions Inuyama-Yuen Station (JNTO). The official castle pages checked for this guide do not print a walking time from Inuyama-Yuen; Meitetsu’s kippu page says trains from the Meitetsu Nagoya direction can be used as far as Inuyama-Yuen.

If you need directions or local information on arrival, the Inuyama tourism association lists its address as 4-21 Matsumoto-cho, Inuyama (TEL 0568-61-2825) on its official site (inuyama.gr.jp). Confirm the current information-centre hours on that site before you rely on them.

Inside the keep: shoes, stairs and bags

Before you climb, note these rules from the official Japanese guidance page (https://inuyama-castle.jp/guidance/):

  • No shoes inside the keep. Put on the shoe covers provided and carry your shoes with you.
  • Steep stairs. The keep has narrow, steep stairs from the basement levels up to the fourth floor. Wear clothes you can climb in and keep your hands free.
  • No large bags. Large luggage can damage the keep and isn't allowed inside. Coin lockers are available.
  • No pets (https://inuyamajo.jp/visit/openinghours-and-entrancefee/index.html).

JNTO describes an observation platform on the top floor with views over the Kiso River and the old town (https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1246/).

Around the castle: castle town, Urakuen and shrines

A castle visit fits well with a walk through the surrounding area. These spots appear on official tourism pages:

  • Inuyama castle town: listed among Inuyama's classic sightseeing spots, with year-round rickshaw rides offered (https://inuyama.gr.jp/).
  • Urakuen: a Japanese garden with the Joan teahouse. Meitetsu’s official Inuyama Jokamachi Kippu page (checked 27 November 2026) lists adult admission as 1,500 yen, or 1,100 yen with the kippu’s garden discount voucher. Hours there are 9:30–17:00, last entry 16:30; tea service 10:00–16:30 (last reception 16:00). Closed days on that page include most Wednesdays, with dated exceptions, plus other closed dates such as 10–11 October, 8–12 December, and from 29 December into early January 2027 (Meitetsu kippu page). Recheck before you go. Several castle set tickets also include Urakuen.
  • Sanko Inari Shrine: known for its red torii gates (https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1246/).
  • Haritsuna Shrine: said to be where the Inuyama Festival began (https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1246/).
  • Inuyama Artifacts Museum and Karakuri Exhibition Room: JNTO says both are free with castle admission (JNTO). Confirm the current arrangement with the official castle pages, because this guide did not re-check a castle page that restates that combination.
  • Kiso River cormorant fishing (Kisogawa Ukai): listed on the official tourism site (inuyama.gr.jp). This guide does not copy a season calendar; check that site for current dates.

Driving and parking

The Japanese access page gives driving routes from several expressway interchanges, including Komaki IC and Gifu-Kakamigahara IC. Most take about 25 minutes. It's about 50 minutes from the Sakae area and about 90 minutes from Chubu Centrair (https://inuyama-castle.jp/access/). Airport arrivals can see Chubu Centrair Airport to Nagoya.

Inuyama Castle No. 1 Car Park (city-run), per the official Japanese access page (https://inuyama-castle.jp/access/):

  • Cars: 300 yen per hour (500 yen per hour on specified days)
  • Daily maximum: 1,800 yen (3,000 yen on specified days)
  • Large buses: 1,000 yen per hour (1,500 yen on specified days)

Which days count as “specified days” is listed on the official parking or tourism notices; this guide does not copy a calendar. Check those notices for your date.

A simple day plan

This outline uses only the official opening times. It doesn't include train times, so check Meitetsu's timetable for those (https://www.meitetsu.co.jp/train/timetable/).

  1. Morning: take the Meitetsu Inuyama Line from Meitetsu Nagoya (Platform 1 for the Inuyama direction) to Inuyama Station. The trip takes around 25 minutes. If you're using the Inuyama Josai-machi Kippu, buy it before boarding.
  2. Mid-morning: walk to the castle, about 20 minutes, and visit the keep soon after the 9:00 opening, before it gets busy.
  3. Midday: explore the castle town, then Sanko Inari and Haritsuna shrines.
  4. Afternoon: visit Urakuen. Check its hours and closed days first.
  5. Late afternoon: if you saved the castle for later, remember last entry is 16:30. Then head back to Nagoya.

Phone navigation helps on the walk from the station. See Japan eSIM for Central Japan.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get from Nagoya to Inuyama Castle?
Official pages give around 25 minutes on the Meitetsu Inuyama Line from Meitetsu Nagoya to Inuyama Station, then about 20 minutes on foot.
How much is Inuyama Castle admission?
1,000 yen for adults and 200 yen for elementary and junior high school students. Preschool children are free.
What time is last entry?
16:30. The castle is open 9:00–17:00 and closed 29–31 December.
Is there a discount ticket from Nagoya?
Yes. Meitetsu's Inuyama Josai-machi Kippu costs 1,950 yen from Meitetsu Nagoya. It includes a round-trip rail ticket, castle admission, an Urakuen discount and castle-town coupons. It's adults only and is sold until 28 February 2027.
How much is the regular Meitetsu fare from Nagoya to Inuyama?
No official Meitetsu or castle page checked for this guide prints a single figure. Use Meitetsu's official fare search.
