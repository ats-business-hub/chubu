Side trips and add-ons (official)

Use these if you add Kenrokuen tickets, Ise, the Toyota museum, Matsumoto Castle, Kamikochi, or the Shinhotaka Ropeway to the loop.

  • Kenrokuen (official)Hours and admission for Kanazawa’s garden. Recheck before you go.
  • Ise Jingu (official)The shrine grounds are free to enter. Check hours and visitor notes on the official site.
  • Toyota Commemorative Museum of Industry and Technology (official)Hours, tickets, and closure days. The museum is typically closed on Mondays.
  • Matsumoto Castle (official)Hours and tickets for the castle, a side trip from Takayama or Nagano.
  • Kamikochi (official)Open mid-April to mid-November only. Private cars are not allowed; use the official access and bus notes.
  • Shinhotaka Ropeway fares (official)Official fares and hours from Takayama / Okuhida. Service can stop for weather or maintenance.
