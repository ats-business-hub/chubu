Highway buses from Nagoya, and arriving via Tokyo

Japan Bus Tickets is a third-party English site for reserved highway buses, including Nagoya–Takayama and Nagoya–Kanazawa. Prefer highwaybus.com or the official operator first, and compare the total, including any booking fee. It does not sell the Nohi Takayama–Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa village bus — use Japan Bus Online or Nohi for that. Airport Taxi is a private transfer for Tokyo airports (Haneda and Narita) and some others (Kansai, Itami, New Chitose, Naha, and Oita). It does not serve Chubu Centrair (NGO); use the Meitetsu train or a Centrair transfer below if you land in Chubu.
