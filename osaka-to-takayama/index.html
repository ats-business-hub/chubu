Osaka or Kyoto to Takayama

Official Nohi highway-bus times and fares, checked 25 November 2026, plus the train alternative via Nagoya.

I created the paid guide mentioned here. Official operator pages remain the primary place to book. The Takayama–Hokuriku Area Pass does not cover this highway bus or the Tokaido Shinkansen.

You can ride a reserved highway bus from Osaka or Kyoto straight to Takayama, or you can go by train via Nagoya. The bus is simpler. The train can be faster if you use the Tokaido Shinkansen — but that bullet train is not included in the Takayama–Hokuriku Area Pass. Times and fares below are from Nohi’s official English Osaka/Kyoto line page, rechecked on 25 November 2026.

The short version

  • Nohi Bus and Kintetsu Bus run the reserved Takayama–Kyoto/Osaka line. Official required time: 5 hours 40 minutes. Official adult one-way fares: Osaka–Takayama 7,000 yen; Kyoto–Takayama 6,500 yen; Osaka–Gujo-Hachiman 6,000 yen; Kyoto–Gujo-Hachiman 5,500 yen.
  • On the official English timetable, three buses a day run in each direction. Reservations open one month ahead (the same day of the prior month). All seats are reserved. The official page lists telephone reservations only (Nohi and Kintetsu Kosoku).
  • The Takayama–Hokuriku Area Pass does not cover this highway bus, and it does not cover the Tokaido Shinkansen from Kyoto or Shin-Osaka toward Nagoya. If you hold that pass, use the conventional JR route on the official map, or pay a separate Shinkansen fare, or pay this bus fare.
  • If you land at Kansai Airport, get into Osaka or Kyoto first, then take this bus or a rail connection. Airport Taxi offers private transfers at Kansai; it does not serve Chubu Centrair.

Bus or train?

  • Direct reserved bus. Officially 5 hours 40 minutes, 7,000 yen from Osaka or 6,500 yen from Kyoto. No transfer. Good if you have luggage and want one ticket.
  • Tokaido Shinkansen to Nagoya, then the Limited Express Hida or the Nohi Nagoya–Takayama bus. This is usually quicker than the direct bus, but you change in Nagoya. Add up official JR and bus fares for your date; I have not invented a combined total here. See Nagoya to Takayama for the second leg. The Area Pass does not include the Tokaido Shinkansen.
  • Area Pass holders starting in Osaka or Kyoto. Use conventional JR on the official Area Pass map (slower), go via Tsuruga and the Hokuriku Shinkansen toward Kanazawa, or pay a separate Shinkansen or highway-bus ticket into the loop.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the highway bus from Osaka or Kyoto to Takayama?
Nohi’s official English page lists adult one-way fares of 7,000 yen from Osaka and 6,500 yen from Kyoto to Takayama Bus Terminal, and 6,000 / 5,500 yen to Gujo-Hachiman I.C. Recheck before you travel.
How long does the bus take?
The official page states a required time of 5 hours 40 minutes. On the official timetable checked on 25 November 2026, the morning buses from Namba at 7:30 and 8:30 arrive in Takayama at 13:22 and 14:22; the afternoon Kintetsu bus from Namba at 14:20 arrives at 20:12.
Does the Takayama–Hokuriku Area Pass cover the Osaka or Kyoto highway bus?
No. Official Area Pass buses are the listed Shirakawa-go / Kanazawa / Toyama lines, not this Osaka/Kyoto highway bus. The pass also does not include the Tokaido Shinkansen.
Is there a faster way than the bus?
Often yes: the Tokaido Shinkansen to Nagoya, then the Limited Express Hida or the Nohi Nagoya–Takayama bus. You change in Nagoya. Compare official fares for your date. See the Nagoya to Takayama guide for that second leg.
How do I book the official bus?
Nohi’s official English page lists telephone reservations only: Nohi Bus Reservation Center 0577-32-1688 (9:00–17:00, Japanese only) and Kintetsu Kosoku Reservation Center 0570-001631 (9:00–19:00). Reservations open one month ahead. If a third-party English site lists your date, compare the total with the official fare before you pay.
