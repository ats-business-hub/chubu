Takayama to Shirakawa-go bus: winter day trip and booking

About 50 minutes on the official highway bus. From 1 December 2026, reserved Takayama–Shirakawa-go services return, and some Kanazawa buses skip the village.

The official way from Takayama to Shirakawa-go (Ogimachi) is the Nohi / partner highway bus. Nohi’s English page says the ride takes about 50 minutes, with 16 round-trip services a day, and that some services are reservation-only. There is no need for a packaged day tour if you only want the bus.

Fares (official)

One-way adult fares published on Nohi’s official English page and fare-revision notice, rechecked 26 November 2026. Units are Japanese yen. Child rules are on the same official English page: a child under 6 rides free, but a child fare is charged if the child occupies a seat; one child is free for one accompanying person, and if the bus is full the operator may refuse a child a seat (riding on a lap is allowed). Recheck before you travel.

From 1 December 2026 the timetable also changes. Nohi’s revision notice says a reserved Takayama Bus Terminal to Shirakawa-go service is restored, and a new Takayama–Kanazawa direct bus is added that does not stop at Shirakawa-go. If you are going to the village, confirm that your booking is the Shirakawa-go line, not the new direct Kanazawa bus. On the official 1 December 2026 timetable PDF, some Kanazawa-bound departures from Takayama are shown passing the village without an arrival time — treat those as the direct bus.

The official 1 December 2026 timetable PDF also says that from 29 December to 3 January (and 13–15 August) the line runs on the weekend and holiday timetable, that some trips (printed in red on that PDF) do not run on weekends, holidays, or those dates, that the bus does not stop at Hatogaya or Iijima on those dates, and that Gokayama Suginami is served only from 1 April to 30 November. Recheck the PDF or the English timetable on the day you travel; this page does not reproduce every departure.

A winter day trip from Takayama

Nohi’s English page says the ride from Takayama Nohi Bus Center to Shirakawa-go takes about 50 minutes, with 16 round-trip services a day, some of them reservation-only. A same-day visit from Takayama is therefore possible on paper. It is still a researched reading of the official timetable, not a trip I have taken.

On the official 1 December 2026 timetable PDF, examples of reserved or village-stopping morning departures from Takayama Bus Terminal include 7:50 arriving at Shirakawa-go (Ogimachi) at 8:40, and 8:50 arriving at 9:40. Return times change by date and by whether the trip runs on a weekday or a holiday timetable, so pick the return from the official page, not from memory. Nohi warns that delays of two hours or more can occur because of car-park queues at the village, heavy snow, or expressway closures — and that not only arrivals but also departures can run late. Leave a wide gap before any train or the last bus back to Takayama.

Clothing is a separate constraint. Official winter advice is in What to wear in Shirakawa-go and Takayama in winter. The village manners page still applies in snow: this is a living village, night sightseeing is for overnight guests only, and there is no tourist parking inside the gassho settlement.

If the bus is full

  1. Check the official timetable for a later or earlier departure the same day. Reserved services should be booked in advance on Japan Bus Online or by phone. Unreserved services can still fill; Nohi says mobile tickets are for non-reserved buses only.
  2. Do not “fix” a full village bus by booking the new Takayama–Kanazawa direct bus. From 1 December 2026 that bus does not stop at Shirakawa-go.
  3. If a Kanazawa–Shirakawa-go seat is still open, that is a different official corridor — see Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go. A same-day Kanazawa–village–Kanazawa sprint is still a poor fit with how the village asks visitors to behave.
  4. Overnight in the village, if you can book lodging, is the official way to be there after dark. The village says night sightseeing is for overnight guests only.
  5. If you are still in or can return to Nagoya, the separate Shirakawa-go day trip from Nagoya page covers that approach, including a guided tour option.

Japan Bus Tickets, an affiliate on other Chubu pages, sells reserved highway buses such as Nagoya–Takayama and Nagoya–Kanazawa. It does not sell the Shirakawa-go village bus, so this page does not use it to book Takayama–Shirakawa-go. Use it only if you still need to reach Takayama or Kanazawa first.

How to book (official first)

  1. Read today’s timetable and the reservation mark on the official Nohi English page. Services marked for reservation should be booked in advance.
  2. Reserve on Japan Bus Online, booking each leg separately (Takayama to Shirakawa-go, and the return). You can also phone the Nohi Bus Reservation Center (9:00am–5:00pm, TEL 0577-32-1688) as listed on the official page.
  3. If you hold the Takayama–Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass, check the official pass page: it covers this corridor’s Nohi / partner buses with some exceptions, and reservations are still required. It is not the nationwide Japan Rail Pass.

Nohi warns that buses may be delayed by two hours or more because of car-park queues at the village, heavy snow, or expressway closures. Build slack, especially in winter, and do not cut the last bus close. Official online reservations for reserved-seating services are listed as 5:00–2:00, via Japan Bus Online, on the English Nohi page. The Nohi Bus Reservation Center is listed as 9:00am–5:00pm, TEL 0577-32-1688.

At the village

The official Shirakawa Village manners page is the rulebook. This is a living village, not a theme park. There is no tourist parking inside the gassho settlement — day visitors arriving by car use the designated lot across the river. The village says the tourist parking lot is open 8am to 5pm (last entry 4:30pm), and that night sightseeing is for overnight guests only. Fire is prohibited except in designated smoking areas; pack your rubbish out; drones are prohibited.

I do not recommend using this bus as a same-day Kanazawa–Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa sprint. The ride exists, but a short stop between two long journeys is a poor fit with how the village asks visitors to behave.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a reservation?
Some services are reservation-only, as marked on the official Nohi English timetable. Book those in advance on Japan Bus Online or by phoning the Nohi reservation centre. Recheck the mark for your departure, including the 1 December 2026 timetable change.
What is the fare from 1 December 2026?
Nohi’s official fare-revision notice lists the adult one-way fare between Takayama Bus Terminal and Shirakawa-go as 2,900 yen from 1 December 2026 (2,800 yen through 30 November). Recheck before you travel.
Could I book the new Takayama–Kanazawa direct bus by mistake?
Yes. From 1 December 2026 Nohi adds a Takayama–Kanazawa direct bus that does not stop at Shirakawa-go. Confirm that your booking is the Shirakawa-go line.
Can I go without a reservation in winter?
Some departures are unreserved, as marked on the official Nohi timetable, and Nohi says convenient mobile tickets are for non-reserved buses only. Other departures are reservation-only. From 1 December 2026, reserved Takayama Bus Terminal to Shirakawa-go services are restored. Recheck the mark for your exact departure.
What if the bus is full?
Try another official departure the same day, or the Kanazawa corridor if seats remain. Do not book the new direct Takayama–Kanazawa bus expecting a village stop. Japan Bus Tickets does not sell the Shirakawa-go village bus.
