Tokyo to Takayama by highway bus or train

Official Nohi Shinjuku-line times and fares, checked 25 November 2026. The overnight bus is seasonal only. Updated 25 November 2026: Nohi’s ¥500 web discount note added.

Official operator pages remain the primary place to book. The Takayama–Hokuriku Area Pass does not cover this highway bus or travel from Tokyo.

From Tokyo, the reserved Nohi / Keio highway bus runs to Hirayu Onsen and Takayama in a stated 5 hours 30 minutes. The train alternative is the Tokaido Shinkansen to Nagoya, then the Limited Express Hida or the Nohi Nagoya bus. Times and fares below are from Nohi’s official English Shinjuku line page, rechecked on 25 November 2026.

The short version

  • Official adult fares: daytime bus 8,000 yen to Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal, or 8,500 yen to Bus Terminal Tokyo Yaesu; overnight bus (seasonal only) 9,000 yen to Shinjuku, or 9,500 yen to Yaesu. The same fares apply from Takayama Bus Terminal or Hirayu Onsen.
  • Web discount: Nohi’s official Shinjuku line page states, “To take advantage of the Web Discount(¥500), please make your reservation via a booking site that offers Japanese language support.” (Nohi, checked 25 November 2026)
  • Official required time: 5 hours 30 minutes. All seats are reserved. Book from one month ahead. Official online booking is via highwaybus.com; unpaid reservations are cancelled automatically if payment is not completed within 3 days (24:00 JST from the reservation date, including that day) and/or 1 day before departure.
  • The overnight bus is not year-round. On the official 2026 table, the Shinjuku 22:55 departure (arriving Takayama at 5:00) runs only 13–14 April, 24 April–5 May, and 10 July–10 October 2026. It is not running in late November or December 2026.
  • The Takayama–Hokuriku Area Pass does not cover this bus and does not cover travel from Tokyo. A nationwide JR Pass can make sense if this ride sits inside a wider Japan rail trip; it still will not cover the highway bus.

Bus or train?

  • Direct reserved bus from Shinjuku (or some trips from Yaesu). Officially 5 hours 30 minutes and 8,000 yen daytime to Shinjuku. One ticket, and you can alight at Hirayu Onsen if that is your plan. Kamikochi, reached from Hirayu, is closed until 17 April 2027; see the Kamikochi from Takayama note before you plan a valley day.
  • Tokaido Shinkansen to Nagoya, then the Hida or the Nagoya–Takayama highway bus. Usually faster, with a change in Nagoya. Compare official JR and bus fares for your date; I have not invented a combined total. See Nagoya to Takayama.
  • Passes. The Area Pass is not valid from Tokyo. A nationwide JR Pass is worth pricing only if you are already riding a lot of JR (Tokyo plus Kyoto or Osaka, for example). It does not cover this highway bus.

If you land at Haneda or Narita

Get into central Tokyo first, then take the Shinjuku (or Yaesu) bus, or the Shinkansen from Tokyo or Shinagawa toward Nagoya. Airport Taxi offers private transfers at Haneda and Narita; compare the quote with official airport trains (Keikyu, Tokyo Monorail, Keisei, or Narita Express) before you book. It does not serve Chubu Centrair. If you fly into Centrair instead, use Centrair Airport to Takayama.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the highway bus from Tokyo to Takayama?
Nohi’s official English page lists daytime adult fares of 8,000 yen to Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal and 8,500 yen to Bus Terminal Tokyo Yaesu, and overnight fares of 9,000 and 9,500 yen. The same fares apply from Takayama Bus Terminal or Hirayu Onsen. Recheck before you travel.
Is there an overnight bus from Shinjuku?
Yes, but only on official seasonal dates in 2026: from Tokyo, the 22:55 Shinjuku departure (arriving Takayama at 5:00) is listed for 13–14 April, 24 April–5 May, and 10 July–10 October 2026. It is not listed as a late-November or December 2026 service.
How long does it take?
The official page states a required time of 5 hours 30 minutes. On the official timetable, a 7:05 Shinjuku departure arrives at Takayama Bus Terminal at 12:50; the last regular daytime bus at 16:05 arrives at 21:50.
Does the Takayama–Hokuriku Area Pass cover this bus?
No. The Area Pass is a regional pass for the official Nagoya/Osaka–Takayama–Shirakawa-go–Kanazawa map. It does not cover Tokyo, and this Shinjuku highway bus is not on the listed bus routes.
Is the train faster?
Usually, yes: the Tokaido Shinkansen to Nagoya, then the Limited Express Hida or the Nagoya–Takayama highway bus. You change in Nagoya. Compare official fares for your date. See the Nagoya to Takayama guide for the second leg.
