Pocket WiFi for a New Year trip to Central Japan: booking, pickup and return around the holidays

A planning checklist for year-end and New Year trips to Nagoya, Takayama, Shirakawa-go and Kanazawa, built from NINJA WiFi official pages read on 28 November 2026. Not a hands-on test.

Traveling to Nagoya, Takayama, Shirakawa-go or Kanazawa over the year-end and New Year holidays? Choosing a pocket WiFi is the easy part. The harder part is timing: when to book, where to pick the device up, and how to return it. This page is a planning checklist built from NINJA WiFi's official pages as displayed on 28 November 2026 (Japan time). No speed or coverage tests were run, and I have not used the service. Plans, prices and pickup locations change, so check the official page before you book.

Pocket WiFi or eSIM?

If you have not decided yet, start with Japan eSIM vs pocket Wi-Fi for Chubu and Japan eSIM for Central Japan.

  • A pocket WiFi can be shared by a group or family, and it suits phones that cannot take an eSIM. NINJA WiFi's plan page describes a device that "can be used by multiple people and devices".
  • An eSIM has nothing to pick up or return, but it needs an unlocked, eSIM-compatible phone. NINJA WiFi also sells eSIM plans (NINJA WiFi eSIM plans, affiliate link).

Step 1: Book early and write down the cut-off times

NINJA WiFi's official pages give different deadlines depending on how you collect the device (as displayed on 28 November 2026):

  • Smart Pickup (locker) at Chubu Centrair Terminal 1: the web application deadline is 3:00 p.m. Japan time, two days before the pickup date.
  • Delivery to your accommodation: order by 14:00 Japan time one day before the delivery date (two days before for Hokkaido and Okinawa).
  • Airport counters, general rule: the plan and step pages say applications can be made through the form up to one day in advance. A separate FAQ answer says that for a next-day pickup you should apply by 16:00 Japan time on the day before, but the airports it names are Narita, Haneda, Kansai, Naha and Fukuoka. Chubu Centrair is not named in that answer, so do not assume next-day pickup is possible there. Book earlier.

Before you book, write down your arrival airport and time, the date you need the device from, your last day in Japan, and how many people and devices will share it. The booking pages are on NINJA WiFi's site: NINJA WiFi rates and plans (affiliate link).

Step 2: Pick a pickup method that fits your arrival time

NINJA WiFi lists three ways to collect a device: an airport counter, Smart Pickup, or delivery to a hotel or other address. For Central Japan the official pages show:

Airport counter pickup and return are free according to the same page. These pages list regular hours and do not show separate year-end or New Year hours, so the hours for 31 December and 1 January are not confirmed here. Check with NINJA WiFi before you rely on a counter on those days. NINJA WiFi's phone support (+81-50-8882-5774) is listed as open 10:00 to 19:00 Japan time, Monday to Friday only, so it is worth asking any questions before the holiday break starts.

If you land late at night, a counter that closes at 10:00 p.m. may not help, so compare the Smart Pickup slots and hotel delivery. Hotel delivery depends on the hotel accepting a parcel before you check in, so ask the hotel directly.

Step 3: Plan the return before you leave

NINJA WiFi's step page says you can return the device at the airport counter, by delivery, or at the airport return box. Points from the official pages:

  • Return the full set (device, cables, adapters, manuals) inside the pouch. The page says equipment is not considered returned unless it is inside the pouch.
  • At the Chubu Centrair Terminal 1 return box, a device returned between 10:01 p.m. and 6:29 a.m. is treated as returned on the previous day. Komatsu has the same rule between 6:31 p.m. and 9:14 a.m.
  • For delivery returns, you can hand the device in at a convenience store or a Yamato Transport counter. Confirm the last collection time for your location in advance: if the package is not collected until the next day, an extra 1,617 yen per day applies, according to the page.
  • The plan page also states that an extension without prior notice is charged 1,617 yen per extra day, and that plans priced above that (including unlimited plans) are charged their regular rate for excess days.

If you fly out of a different airport than you arrived at (for example, arriving in Nagoya and leaving from Komatsu or Tokyo), check that your return method works from there.

Step 4: Keep the device charged in the cold

In snowy areas such as Takayama and Shirakawa-go, many battery-powered devices lose charge faster in cold weather. This page has no measurements, so treat that as a general habit rather than a spec. Keep the router in an inside pocket, carry a power bank and short cable, and charge everything overnight. NINJA WiFi lists a portable 5,500 mAh spare battery at 110 yen per day (tax included) among its options. Step 5: Know where you will need data most

On a typical New Year route, a connection helps for checking bus and train times (see Central Japan snow route), confirming highway buses between Takayama, Shirakawa-go and Kanazawa (Takayama to Shirakawa-go bus, Kanazawa to Shirakawa-go), and maps and translation at shrines and markets. Coverage in mountain areas can differ from city coverage, and I have not measured it. NINJA WiFi's plan page names the carrier network for each device (for example SoftBank 4G LTE for one model and docomo 5G for another) and says speed is limited after you exceed the plan's daily data allowance. Read the plan notes for the model you choose.

Quick checklist

  • Decide pocket WiFi or eSIM
  • Check the cut-off time for your pickup method (Smart Pickup: 3:00 p.m. two days before)
  • Confirm counter or locker hours for your arrival date, including 31 December and 1 January
  • Ask your hotel about accepting a delivery, if you choose that option
  • Confirm how and where to return the device on your departure day
  • Pack a power bank for cold days

Frequently asked questions

When is the booking deadline for NINJA WiFi at Chubu Centrair?
NINJA WiFi's official page says Smart Pickup (locker) orders at Chubu Centrair Terminal 1 must be placed on the web by 3:00 p.m. Japan time two days before pickup. Delivery to accommodation must be ordered by 14:00 Japan time one day before. The general plan page says the form accepts applications up to one day in advance. Pages read on 28 November 2026; check the live page before you book.
What are the NINJA WiFi counter hours at Chubu Centrair Terminal 1?
The official page lists the Terminal 1, 2F Access Plaza counter as open 6:30 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., with a return box. It does not show separate year-end or New Year hours, so confirm 31 December and 1 January with NINJA WiFi before you rely on the counter.
Can I return a NINJA WiFi device after the counter closes?
The official page says a return box is available. At Chubu Centrair Terminal 1, a device returned between 10:01 p.m. and 6:29 a.m. is counted as returned on the previous day. Return the full set of equipment inside the pouch.
Is NINJA WiFi phone support open on weekends?
NINJA WiFi's site lists support hours as 10:00 to 19:00 Japan time, Monday to Friday only. Ask any questions before the holiday break starts.
