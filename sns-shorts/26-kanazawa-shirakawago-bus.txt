The Kanazawa–Shirakawa-go highway bus is a reserved seat on an official operator, not a packaged day tour. Book it as a bus. Do not assume a tour voucher is the same product.

Prefer Japan Bus Online or the official operator first. Recheck the date, the stop, and whether the seat is reserved.
