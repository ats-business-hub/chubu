Not every day trip from Nagoya is the same length. Inuyama Castle is the easiest half day. Magome–Tsumago is the walking day, if you start early. Takayama or Shirakawa-go is possible, but it is a long day — check official last buses before you go.

Ghibli Park is a separate plan: official tickets first. Recheck hours and seats for your date.
