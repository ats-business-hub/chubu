The nationwide Japan Rail Pass rarely pays off for a Nagoya–Takayama–Kanazawa loop. The official Takayama–Hokuriku Area Pass is 19,800 yen for five days (adult). The official nationwide ordinary seven-day Japan Rail Pass is 50,000 yen if you buy it online.

The Area Pass does not cover the Tokaido Shinkansen, or the Nohi Osaka, Kyoto, or Tokyo–Takayama highway buses. Recheck official prices before you buy.
