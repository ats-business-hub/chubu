Osaka to Takayama is an official Nohi highway bus: 7,000 yen from Osaka, 6,500 yen from Kyoto, about 5 hours 40 minutes. The Takayama–Hokuriku Area Pass does not cover this bus, or the Tokaido Shinkansen.

The official Nohi page lists telephone reservations only. Recheck that page before you assume an online seat is available, and compare any other listing with the official fare.
