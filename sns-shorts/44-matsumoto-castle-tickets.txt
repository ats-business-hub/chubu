Matsumoto Castle needs an official timed ticket. The keep has many stairs, and this is not a quick photo stop from the parking area.

Recheck official hours and ticket rules for your date. If you are adding Matsumoto to a winter trip, do not assume Kamikochi is open as a second stop.
