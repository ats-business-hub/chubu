Start in Magome and finish in Tsumago if you can — that direction is less uphill. From Nagoya, take JR to Nakatsugawa, then the official 800-yen cash bus to Magome. The walk is about 8 km and 3–4 hours. The local buses are not covered by the Japan Rail Pass.

From 1 December 2026, official winter luggage forwarding is 2,500 yen per item. Recheck the Tsumago notice before you go.
