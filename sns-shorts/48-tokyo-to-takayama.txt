Tokyo to Takayama by official Nohi daytime highway bus is 8,000 yen from Shinjuku and 8,500 yen from Yaesu, about 5 hours 30 minutes. The overnight bus is seasonal — it is not running in late November and December 2026.

Book on the official operator pages first, then compare. Recheck the date, the departure stop, and whether the overnight service is actually running.
