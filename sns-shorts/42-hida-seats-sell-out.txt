Reserved seats on the Limited Express Hida sell out on busy days. This is not an unreserved hop you can decide at the platform.

Book official seats for your date, especially around weekends, holidays, and winter weekends. If the train is full, the reserved highway bus is the other plan — also booked, not hoped for.
