Chubu Centrair Airport to Takayama: train or bus

Every route goes through Nagoya: about 4,280 yen by bus or 7,570 yen by μSKY and the Hida. Official pages first.

No train or bus runs straight from Chubu Centrair International Airport (NGO) to Takayama. Every public route goes through Nagoya. You take the Meitetsu train to Meitetsu Nagoya, about 28 minutes on the μSKY, then either JR’s Limited Express Hida or the reserved Nohi highway bus. Plan on most of a day if you land after midday. Fares below are adult one-way prices in yen, checked on 25 November 2026.

Step 1: Centrair to Nagoya on Meitetsu

The Meitetsu airport station is next to the terminal. The μSKY reaches Meitetsu Nagoya in about 28 minutes for 980 yen plus a 450-yen μticket (500 yen if you settle on board). A Limited Express takes about 36 minutes, and its ordinary cars need only the 980-yen fare. Full details are in Centrair Airport to Nagoya. Daytime airport buses to Nagoya Station are suspended, so the train is the realistic choice.

Step 2a: JR Limited Express Hida (the predictable option)

From Meitetsu Nagoya, walk to JR Nagoya Station and board the Hida, a direct limited express to Takayama. Ekitan, a Japanese timetable service, lists about 10 round trips a day and a ride of roughly 2½ hours. Its regular-season adult fares are 6,140 yen for a reserved seat, 5,610 yen unreserved, and 8,410 yen in the Green Car. Reserved seats go on sale one month ahead. In JR Central’s designated peak periods every seat is reserved, so an unreserved ticket will not work. The official Takayama–Hokuriku Area Tourist Pass page says that applies from 25 December 2026 to 5 January 2027.

For an evening arrival, check the last Hida of the day on JR Central’s timetable before you leave the airport. If you cannot make it comfortably, sleeping in Nagoya and taking a morning train is usually the calmer plan.

Step 2b: Nohi highway bus (the cheaper option)

Nohi Bus’s official English page for the Takayama–Nagoya line lists a running time of 2 hours 40 minutes. Fares are 3,600 yen, or 3,300 yen when you reserve and pay by card on highwaybus.com. All seats are reserved, and booking opens one month ahead. Toward Takayama you can board at Meitetsu Bus Center (by Meitetsu Nagoya) or at JR Nagoya Station. Nohi’s published Takayama-bound departures from Meitetsu Bus Center run from 7:00 to 20:30. Expect road delays at busy times.

Pre-booked private transfer

Takayama is a long road trip from the airport. For a family or a group with luggage, or a late landing, a fixed quote can still make sense. Going on to Shirakawa-go

Most travellers sleep in Takayama and take the reserved Nohi bus to Shirakawa-go the next morning. See Takayama to Shirakawa-go by bus. If you want to go from Nagoya straight to the village, see Shirakawa-go from Nagoya.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a direct bus or train from Centrair Airport to Takayama?
No. Centrair’s official bus page lists no Takayama route, and there is no direct train. Take Meitetsu to Meitetsu Nagoya (about 28 minutes by μSKY), then JR’s Limited Express Hida or the Nohi highway bus to Takayama.
How much does it cost to get from Centrair to Takayama?
As of 25 November 2026, about 4,280 yen by Meitetsu (980) plus the Nohi highway bus booked online (3,300). By μSKY (1,430 with μticket) plus a reserved Hida seat (6,140 in the regular season, per Ekitan) it is about 7,570 yen. Recheck fares before you travel.
How long does Centrair to Takayama take?
The rides alone add up to about 3 hours: roughly 28 minutes on the μSKY plus about 2½ hours on the Hida, or 2 hours 40 minutes on the bus. Allow extra time for immigration, the transfer at Nagoya, and waiting for your departure.
Should I stay in Nagoya if I land in the evening?
Often, yes. Check the last Hida on JR Central’s timetable and the last Nohi bus (20:30 from Meitetsu Bus Center on its published timetable). If your flight lands late, a night in Nagoya and a morning train is the calmer plan.
